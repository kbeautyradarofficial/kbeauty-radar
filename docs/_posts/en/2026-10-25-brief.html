1. Medicube: +85% in the US, and a bigger jump in Europe

APR says Medicube's US sales during Amazon Prime Big Deal Days (Oct 6–7, 2026) rose about 85% versus last year's event. Thirteen Medicube products were in Amazon US's overall beauty top 100. (Herald Business)

For buyers, Europe is the more interesting part. This was the brand's first Big Deal Days in the UK, Germany, Spain, Italy and France. It averaged 16+ top-100 products per country, up from 7 at June's Prime Day. Medicube was also the #1 Amazon search term in the US, UK, Italy and Spain. Keep in mind that is a search ranking, not a sales ranking.

For context, Econovill reported that 38 of Amazon US's top 100 skincare products during Prime Day (June 23–26, 2026) were Korean, including 7 of the top 10. (Econovill)

For buyers: Korean brands now treat Amazon tentpoles as multi-market events. EU retailers and distributors should expect more competition from brands selling direct on Amazon. Agree promo calendars with Korean suppliers early.

2. Beauty of Joseon reaches 1,263 Boots doors

Goodai Global's Beauty of Joseon is now in 1,263 Boots UK stores (October 2026, company-announced). Seoul Economic Daily says that is more than any other K-beauty brand at Boots. The brand entered Boots in June 2024, so the rollout took about 28 months. (Seoul Economic Daily)

In April 2026 it ranked #5 in sales among ~200 skincare brands at Boots. It was #1 among ~20 K-beauty brands and #2 in Everyday Skin. Only 100 doors, about 8%, have a dedicated display. The company plans to use its UK network to enter other European markets.

For buyers: Boots rolled out doors first and dedicated fixtures second. European drugstores testing K-beauty can start with a narrow hero assortment across many doors before they commit fixture space.

3. L'Oréal Q3: +11.9% reported, +7.2% like-for-like

L'Oréal's Q3 2026 (Jul–Sep) sales were €11.56bn, up 11.9% reported and 7.2% like-for-like. Adjusted for an IT-transition timing effect, LFL growth was 7.7%. (Il Sole 24 ORE) FashionNetwork reports nine-month sales of about €35.34bn, up 6.9% LFL, and says Q3 beat a 5.9% consensus. (FashionNetwork)

For buyers: Amorepacific and LG H&H report Q3 in the coming weeks. Many Korean headlines quote the 11.9% reported figure, so compare like-for-like with like-for-like.

4. Dr.Groot: Costco as a back door to Mexico

LG H&H's Dr.Groot has been sold in 682 Costco stores across the US, Canada and Mexico since October 6, 2025. LG H&H said its North American sales rose more than 790% in January–July 2025. (SmartToday) Youth Daily reported that Dr.Groot launched in ~90 Sephora US stores, with a rollout to ~400 planned for August 2026. (Youth Daily) A cumulative sales figure is circulating in Korean headlines, but we could not find a primary source for it, so it is not included here.

For buyers: LatAm distributors can treat Mexico Costco sell-through as a demand signal before local distribution deals are set.
