Bottom line

VT Cosmetics' consolidated revenue for Q2 2026 was KRW 131.7B, up 18.1% from KRW 111.5B a year earlier. Operating profit was KRW 10.7B (Asia Economy). Beauty revenue was KRW 123.9B (+20%), 94% of the total (eToday). Three things moved this quarter: the regional mix, the hero lineup and the margin structure.

1. Europe and the Middle East: one retailer at a time

Q2 revenue in Europe grew 454%, as B2B accounts such as Derma Space and Notino started contributing at scale. Middle East revenue grew 546%, led by Gratis in Türkiye (Asia Economy). Russia/CIS grew 236%.

These rates start from a small base. Q2 regional shares were Japan 26%, US 11%, Russia/CIS 7%, Europe 6% and Greater China 3% (eToday). The method is the takeaway: secure one anchor retailer per region instead of hunting for a distributor in every country.

2. PDRN is the new hero line

  • Daiso: Q2 sales rose about 65% YoY on the PDRN glow and body care lines. eToday reports this on a sell-in basis.
  • Olive Young: Q2 sales rose about 47% QoQ on a Pokémon IP collaboration. The PDRN sun care product ranked No. 1 in both the sun care category and the overall ranking.
  • Costco US: a PDRN duo pack was added in Q2. The Reedle Shot duo pack sold out, and more stock ships in Q3 (Asia Economy).

3. Channel footprint

VT is in two Olive Young US stores and on Olive Young's US online store, runs about 120 SKUs on Olive Young Global, and is stocked in 23,058 stores in Japan (as of the August 2026 earnings release, eToday). According to a press release, VT entered about 1,410 Ulta Beauty stores in July 2025 with nine products, including the Reedle Shot series and a vegan PDRN essence (PR Newswire).

For reference, Q3 2025 US revenue was KRW 14.9B (+372%), including KRW 7.0B from Amazon and KRW 2.6B from TikTok Shop. Japan revenue fell 10% to KRW 35.7B in the same quarter (Pinpoint News, citing Kyobo Securities). At 26% of Q2 2026 sales, Japan is still VT's largest market.

4. Growth vs. margin

H1 2026 consolidated revenue was KRW 246.0B and operating profit KRW 28.0B; beauty revenue grew 17% to KRW 231.4B (eToday). An IR analysis by ChickStock reports H1 operating profit fell 51.8%, from KRW 58.1B to KRW 28.0B, and operating margin dropped from 27.4% to 11.4%. The same analysis says ad and promotion spend rose 119%, from KRW 16.4B to KRW 36.1B. Q3 results will show whether that spend pays off. Partners should expect promotion terms to shift. In August 2026 VT also said it will buy back and cancel KRW 15B of its own shares.

What buyers should watch

  • Whether Daiso's sell-in turns into sell-through, measured by reorder cadence
  • How PDRN claims are substantiated as competing SKUs multiply
  • Whether Japan, still VT's largest market, returns to growth
  • Whether Q3 marketing efficiency recovers

Sources: Asia Economy, eToday, ChickStock, Pinpoint News, PR Newswire
