1. Korea's ODMs: one beat, two misses

The factories behind most K-beauty brands had very different quarters. What decided it was where each one sits in its capacity build-out.

  • Cosmax reportedly booked Q3 2026 consolidated sales of KRW 875.8bn (+49.6% YoY) and operating profit of KRW 83.3bn (+94.9%), 3.8% above the KRW 80.3bn consensus. (SBS Biz)
  • Its Korea unit passed KRW 600bn in quarterly sales for the first time (KRW 616.1bn, +61%). Part of that is a soft comparison: Q3 2025 operating profit had fallen 13%. The US unit was profitable for a second straight quarter. (Newspim)
  • Kolmar Korea grew sales 21% to KRW 828.2bn. Operating profit of KRW 81.9bn missed the KRW 91.6bn consensus as fixed costs rose. (The Public)
  • Cosmecca Korea grew sales 36% to KRW 247.5bn, but operating profit was flat at KRW 27.2bn (consensus KRW 31.2bn) and its margin fell 3.9pp to 11.0%. (The Public)

For buyers: ODMs with squeezed margins may push for higher MOQs or new pricing. If you source private label or exclusive SKUs from Korea, confirm lead times and pricing for 1H 2027 deliveries now.

2. APR: sales doubled, profit grew 69%

  • APR reported preliminary Q3 2026 sales of KRW 773.1bn (+100.3%) and operating profit of KRW 160.6bn (+68.9%) under a new accounting basis. On the old basis, operating profit would have been KRW 176.7bn. (SBS Biz)
  • About 94% of Q3 sales came from overseas (KRW 722.9bn, +135%). North America was 46% of the total and Europe 23%. Jan–Sep 2026 sales of KRW 2.134tn already exceed APR's full-year 2025 sales. (Wikitree)

For buyers: Profit grew about 31 points slower than sales, so scaling in North America is costing margin. Fast-growing Korean brands will favour retail partners that lower their marketing cost per unit. Retail media packages and in-store visibility are your strongest bargaining chips.

3. Olive Young heads to US suburban malls

  • The first US store opened in Pasadena on May 29, 2026 with about 400 brands and 5,000 products. In its first three days, more than 1,000 social posts drew 8M+ views. (CJ Newsroom)
  • Five more California stores are reportedly planned by 1H 2027: Irvine Spectrum, Del Amo, Bloom on 3rd (LA), Brea Mall and Fashion Valley in San Diego, its first store outside the LA area. That would bring the US total to 7. (Korea JoongAng Daily)
  • Three of the five are Simon Property Group malls. Olive Young has not disclosed US store sales or how much it is investing. (Korea JoongAng Daily)

For buyers: A dedicated K-beauty chain is moving into mainstream Southern California malls. US retailers should expect more competition for exclusive Korean brands and launch windows in the region.

4. A K-content hit gives you about three months

  • A KITA analysis (data from Jul 2021 to Jun 2026) reportedly found that when K-pop popularity runs 10% above expectations, Korean cosmetics exports to Mexico rise 1.7% in the same month, 1.6% after three months and 1.3% after six months. (Financial News)
  • When K-drama popularity rose 10%, Vietnam's imports of Korean cosmetics rose 0.7% in the same month. (Newspim)
  • The study covered Germany, Mexico, Japan and Vietnam. It recommends concentrating promotion in the first ~3 months of a hit. (Asia Economy)

For buyers: In Mexico, LATAM distributors can plan stock for a lift that lasts months, not days. Keep reorder slots open so you can move within weeks of a breakout show or comeback.

Context: export volume

Korean cosmetics exports reportedly reached about USD 11.13bn in Jan–Sep 2026 (+31.0% YoY). That is already about 98% of full-year 2025 (USD 11.42bn), with the US and Europe driving the growth. (CNC News)
