1. Missha: 75% overseas, but part of the margin gain came from cost cuts

Able C&C, which owns Missha, reported Q2 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 65.2bn and operating profit of KRW 10.4bn, a margin of about 16%. Overseas sales were 75% of revenue and grew 38% YoY. US sales rose 154%, and Europe rose 48% in its best quarter yet. (Economist Korea)

The shift was fast. Reports put the overseas share at 52% in Q1 2025 and 70% in Q1 2026 (Bizwatch). The company set its 75% target for 2026 in December 2025, when the share was 63% (Dealsite).

Total revenue grew much less. According to Insight, reported revenue rose about 5%, or 24.5% if closed domestic stores are excluded. IB Tomato credits much of the profit gain to closing its own stores, leaving duty-free and cutting staff.

For buyers: Missha now earns most of its revenue abroad, and the US and Europe are where it is growing. Before you commit to deeper listings, ask for sell-out data by market.

2. Korea's pension fund raises its stakes in all three big ODMs

The National Pension Service raised its stakes for "simple investment" purposes to 12.68% in Kolmar Korea, 12.85% in Cosmax and 12.98% in Cosmecca Korea (disclosed July 2026). Q1 2026 results show how far apart their profit growth was (MTN):

  • Kolmar Korea: revenue KRW 728bn (+11.5%), operating profit KRW 78.9bn (+31.6%)
  • Cosmax: revenue KRW 682bn (+16%), operating profit KRW 53bn (+3%)
  • Cosmecca Korea: revenue KRW 185.1bn (+56.4%), operating profit KRW 21.9bn (+78%)

For buyers: Revenue grew at all three, but profit growth ranged from 3% to 78%. If you are developing a private label, confirm lead times and MOQs for H1 2027 with your ODM now.

3. About 90% of Korean sunscreen exports go to Europe

Hana Securities analyst Park Jong-dae says about 90% of Korea's sunscreen exports go to Europe and expects growth to continue into H2 2026 (MTN). Missha's 48% growth in Europe in Q2 fits the same trend.

For buyers: Lock in 2027 sun care volumes early. Before you list, check that each SKU has a CPNP notification and SPF test documentation.

4. Laneige: after Sephora, its own US store?

Laneige is reported to be opening its first official US store. The location and timing are not confirmed. Reports say the brand entered Target in 2014 and was pulled in 2016 after about USD 10m in sales. It then moved to Sephora, launching online in June 2017 and in about 150 US doors that September. (WWD)

For retailers: An owned store gives the brand its own customer data. K-beauty brands that grew through wholesale may push harder on DTC, so watch for changes to exclusives and launch timing.
