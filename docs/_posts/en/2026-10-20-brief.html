1. Record exports, but skincare is doing all the growing

Korea shipped US$11.1B of cosmetics in January–September 2026, up 31.1% year on year, according to provisional MFDS data. That is a nine-month record. It is also only $0.3B short of 2025's full-year total of $11.4B (Electronic Times). Q3 alone came to US$4.17B, up 7.5% on Q2 (KMJ).

The category split matters more than the total. Over the same period, skincare rose 45.1% to US$8.72B. Color cosmetics fell 3.6% to US$1.11B (SBS Biz). Suppliers built around color are missing the boom. A January 2026 broker report says lip products make up about 64% of C&C International's sales (Naver Securities Research).

  • For buyers: put most of your K-beauty open-to-buy into skincare, and buy Korean color selectively, following trends.

2. US +40.6% while Amazon's Holiday Beauty Event is live

The US was the top destination in Jan–Sep 2026, taking US$2.35B (up 40.6%) for a 21.1% share. China was flat at US$1.58B, up 0.4% (Herald Business). Amazon's sixth Holiday Beauty Event runs from October 19 to November 1, with official discounts of up to 30% (About Amazon). Some outlets report flash deals of up to 40%, and NBC lists Medicube among the promoted brands. No one has published K-beauty's share of event sales yet.

  • For retailers competing with Amazon: K-beauty gift sets and exclusive bundles protect margin better than matching discounts on hero SKUs.

3. Goodai Global (₩1.47T revenue) builds its own brands, and ODM capacity may tighten

Goodai Global owns Beauty of Joseon. Its consolidated revenue grew from ₩139.4B in 2023 to ₩1.4718T in 2025, and 2025 operating profit was ₩273.4B. The company is co-developing "Peace Minus One Beauty" with G-Dragon's label PEACEMINUSONE, aiming to launch in 1H 2027 and expand to the US and Japan. It launched the inner-beauty brand Isebi on October 1 and is building the skincare brand Depti as an in-house venture (Sidae). Local press reports that it is preparing an IPO (Seoul Economic TV).

For buyers, the issue is supply. Kolmar and Goodai's joint sun-care products reportedly passed 100M units cumulative as of March 2026 (SmartToday). In August, reports said Goodai had placed an order of roughly 200M units with Kolmar. Indie brands worry this will squeeze ODM capacity (MyDaily).

  • For distributors: confirm Q1–Q2 2027 delivery dates with your indie Korean suppliers now, especially for SPF.

4. AI skin scanning: Olive Young passes 1M uses, Torriden adds a kiosk

ChoiceTech Korea installed its ChoiceDx AI skin-analysis kiosk at Torriden Connect Seongsu, Torriden's Seoul flagship. The kiosk measures skin type, pigmentation, sebum, pores and wrinkles, then recommends Torriden products (Startup Researcher). The same technology runs Olive Young's SKIN SCAN, which passed 1M uses as of January 2026. Results now link to the Olive Young app (KoreaTechDesk).

  • For retailers: Korea's biggest beauty retailer and a leading brand flagship now both run diagnostic-led recommendation. If you pilot it, decide upfront who owns the skin data: you or the brand.
