1. Medicube enters ~400 U.S. Costco stores, with 9,000+ U.S. doors planned by year-end

APR's Medicube started selling in about 400 U.S. Costco stores this month. The company plans to expand to every U.S. Costco store, then move into Costco Canada and Europe in stages. (The Korea Times)

  • Launch SKU: a Costco-only bundle of the Zero Pore Pad. PDRN-line products will follow. Medicube is already in every Costco store in Korea.
  • U.S. retail rollout: Ulta ~1,400 stores (Aug 2025), Target 1,500+ (Apr 2026), Walmart ~3,000 (Jun 2026), Costco ~400 (Oct 2026). About 3,000 CVS stores are planned by the end of 2026.
  • APR's North American sales were KRW 647.9bn in H1 2026. That is up more than 260% year on year and about 30x the 2021 level.
  • APR says the Zero Pore Pad has been No. 1 in Amazon U.S. facial toners and astringents since Feb 2025. That ranking comes from the company itself.

Counting planned stores, that is 9,000+ doors in about 16 months. At this pace the main risk is price, not sell-through. Put an Amazon hero product on club shelves in the same pack and shoppers compare unit prices right away. The Costco-only bundle looks designed to prevent that. More doors also mean bigger opening orders and more working capital.

For buyers: If a brand is also strong on Amazon, ask for a channel-specific pack size or bundle and a written MAP policy before you commit to volume.

2. Missha up 58% during Prime Big Deal Days, holding BB cream #1 and #5

Able C&C said Missha's sales during Amazon U.S. Prime Big Deal Days (Oct 6–7, 2026, U.S. time) rose 58% year on year. Sales were up 46% on day one and 70% on day two. (Newspim)

  • M Perfect Cover BB Cream PRO (red BB) stayed No. 1 in BB cream, as it was last year. Serum BB Cream PRO (purple BB) reached No. 5.
  • Missha ranked 15th in Amazon's overall face makeup category. No other K-beauty brand was reported near the top.
  • Coverage of the 2025 event (Oct 7–8) reported +74% for the BB cream alone (Ezyeconomy). This year's 58% covers the whole brand, so the two numbers can't be compared directly.

Most of the lift looks like it came from owning one narrow category, not from general event traffic. The #15 rank in face makeup is a useful benchmark. On Amazon U.S., K-beauty color cosmetics are still a niche.

For buyers: For Q4 K-makeup assortments, start with SKUs that already lead their sub-category on Amazon. Those are a safer reorder than a broad color range.

3. Indonesia halal deadline: reported 2-year delay, not yet officially confirmed

Indonesia's 2021 Halal Product Assurance Law gave cosmetics a 5-year grace period, which ended on Oct 17, 2026. Uncertified products face either a sales ban or a 'Non-Halal' label. Reports differ on which one applies. Korea's MFDS planned to negotiate with BPJPH on a grace period for goods cleared through customs before the deadline. (Cosinkorea, as of July 2026 reporting)

Beautynury reports that Indonesia has pushed mandatory cosmetics halal certification back by 2 years, to around October 2028. We could not confirm a BPJPH or MFDS notice. (Beautynury)

  • Check with BPJPH and BPOM directly for an official notice.
  • Confirm how stock already cleared through customs will be treated.
  • Ask suppliers whether Non-Halal labeling is an option and how it would affect sell-through.
  • Even with a delay, ODMs that already run halal-certified lines may be the easier way to source.

For distributors: Don't put certification plans on hold because of one report. Stick to the original timeline until an official notice is out.

4. K-Beauty Expo: a record 679 exhibitors, but no deal results yet

KINTEX said the 2026 K-Beauty Expo (Oct 15–17) drew a record 679 exhibitors and 1,088 booths. We could not find 2026 consultation or contract results. (Wikitree)

Reported results for the 2025 edition were US$138M in export consultations, US$43M in contracts in progress and 1,780 consultations. The US$43M was not signed contracts.

For buyers: More booths means more choice, but also more noise. Book meetings with shortlisted brands in advance and ask them for MOQ, lead times and export references at the show.
