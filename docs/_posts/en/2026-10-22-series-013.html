Bottom line: The China rebound is real, but +76% is a 20-day number. For the full month of September 2026, exports to China were $210M, up 13.6% (preliminary data via CNC News). Size orders on final monthly customs data, not on mid-month headlines.

1. One month, two growth rates

  • Kyobo Securities put September 1–20 cosmetics exports to China at about $190M: +76% YoY and +193% vs. the same days in August (Asia Economy).
  • Exports to the US over the same 20 days were $149M, so China was briefly ahead (Korea Economic TV).
  • Full-month September by market: US $260M (+21.9%), China $210M (+13.6%), EU $170M (+110.4%) (CNC News). Over the full month, the US was No. 1 again.
  • Total Korean cosmetics exports hit a monthly record of $1.51B in September (+31.4%) (Ministry of Trade). January–September reached $11.14B (+30.8%), already 98% of full-year 2025.
  • Monthly YoY growth to China in 2026: May +1.2%, June +31.0%, July +18.9%, August +1.7% (Korea Economic TV).

Editor's read: $190M in 20 days and $210M for the month implies almost nothing shipped in the last 10 days. The ministry's release also mentions fewer working days in September, and the two figures may come from different data cuts. Either way, +76% is not a monthly growth rate. The more useful signal is the level: Kyobo says China is back above $200M a month for the first time in about two years. August's +1.7% shows how much the monthly numbers swing.

2. Makeup is leading the rebound in China

  • Makeup exports to China in September 1–20 were 1.6x skincare exports ($57.9M) (Ziksir).
  • Kyobo calls the driver an inbound 'butterfly effect': Chinese tourists buy in Korea, then buy again at home. Chinese visitors to Korea totaled 4,816,134 in January–August, up 29.0% (Dealsite).
  • The brands driving the rebound are newer names that broke out globally first, such as Anua, d'Alba and Medicube, rather than the conglomerates that led China in the past (Dealsite).

3. The reverse flow: C-beauty makeup in Korea

  • Korea imported $62.45M of Chinese cosmetics in January–September 2026 (+24.5%). Total imports grew 1.9%, so China's share rose from 6.9% to 8.4% (Yonhap Infomax via KB Think).
  • Flower Knows joined Musinsa Beauty in February as its first Chinese cosmetics brand, ranked No. 1 in eye makeup during the launch promotion, and is pursuing Olive Young. Its blush costs KRW 28,000, so this is not a budget play.
  • Judydoll sells on Coupang at more than 20% below its price in China.
  • K-beauty makeup exports fell 6% to $164.6M in September and 4.2% in H1. Skincare rose 29% to $595.1M in September.
  • Domestic brands now hold 57% of China's home market, and some Chinese brands are moving production to Korean ODMs to sell as 'K-beauty'.

A Shinhan Investment analyst says there is no direct sales cannibalization yet because the core categories differ, but notes that the timing overlaps. K-makeup is rising in China while C-makeup is rising in Korea.

4. ODMs split on China

  • Kolmar Korea is moving its Beijing plant to Sejong and adding lines there, taking capacity from 370M to over 630M units (CNC News).
  • Cosmax is expanding Shanghai capacity to 1.6B units. With a new Thailand plant (230M units), it is putting over KRW 260B into three new plants, targeting 4B units a year.

Where a China-bound SKU is made changes its lead time, MOQ and origin labeling. Factories are running near capacity, so check Q4 line slots early.

5. Counterfeits and certification

  • MBC reports a surge in fake K-cosmetics, leading to raids on factories in China (Oct 13).
  • Small brands raised Chinese counterfeits as a key problem in a meeting with Minister Lee So-young (Asia Economy, Oct 18).
  • The trade ministry held a briefing in Shanghai on Chinese certification rules for cosmetics and apparel exporters (Newsis, Oct 12).
  • Korea launched a 'K-Brand government certification' system against overseas fakes, with the government acting as rights holder of a national certification mark (The K Beauty Science).

Checklist for buyers and brand teams

  • Size China reorders on final customs data and a 3-month trend
  • Ask brands for Korea tourist sell-through by SKU before building China assortments
  • Benchmark K and C makeup SKUs on price and reviews before Q4 color buys
  • Confirm the ODM production site, line slot, MOQ and lead time for each China-bound SKU
  • Buy only through authorized distributors and ask for trademark and authenticity proof
