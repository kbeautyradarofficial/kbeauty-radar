The takeaway: a quarter of profit, not just volume

In Q2 2026, Amorepacific's overseas revenue grew 28% to ₩551.6bn, and overseas operating profit rose 99% to ₩71.8bn. Overseas margins were in double digits in both Q1 and Q2 (CNC News).

On a group consolidated basis, Amorepacific Holdings reported Q2 revenue of ₩1.2543tn (+14.6%) and operating profit of ₩122.8bn (+53.3%). Domestic revenue rose 10% and domestic profit rose 48%, for a margin of about 10%. Online and MBS channels each grew by double digits. For comparison, Amorepacific Group IR puts full-year 2025 group results at ₩4.6232tn in revenue and ₩368.0bn in operating profit.

There is a caveat. According to a StockHub summary, BNK Investment & Securities puts the overseas margin at 13.0% but expects ad and promotion spending to limit operating leverage through 2027. ODMs, agencies and 3PLs should prepare for pressure on efficiency and unit costs, not bigger budgets.

Channels: Amazon hero SKUs plus D2C reach

  • Insight reports that at Prime Day (June 23–26, 2026), the group's US sales rose 20% YoY. LANEIGE Lip Glowy Balm and Lip Sleeping Mask ranked #1 and #2 in lip balm, and the COSRX Snail Mucin serum ranked #26 in all of Beauty & Personal Care.
  • COSRX ranked #1 in Amazon beauty in Germany and the UK during Prime Day. In Japan, it was named first-half #1 at the Qoo10 Mega Beauty Awards 2026 (CNC News).
  • On October 8, COSRX expanded direct shipping from its official store from mainly the US and Canada to about 200 countries. New markets get dedicated promotions and membership benefits. No sales figures were disclosed (Financial News).

Direct shipping lets COSRX test demand where it has no distributor. For existing resellers, it may also mean more price-gap and gray-market friction.

Before Black Friday

LANEIGE and COSRX results for Prime Big Deal Days (October 6–7) were not public. Rival medicube said its US sales rose about 85% and that 13 of its products made the US Amazon beauty top 100 (MTN). Use the Prime Day lip balm ranks as your reference point, and lock in stock depth and coupons for hero lip SKUs early.

Campaigns and launches

  • Etoday reports that LANEIGE began a Sleeping Mask campaign with BTS's Jin on September 14. The company says the line has sold more than 58.3 million units globally since 2020, so the campaign comes with a proof number attached.
  • CosinKorea reports that the limited-edition Matin Kim collaboration, Juicy Pop Box Lip Tint Monotune, is a pH-reactive tint that looks black and turns purple on the lips. It launched overseas first on September 1 and arrived in Korea in October through Olive Young and other channels. A Musinsa Beauty exclusive pre-launch was reported on October 12.

Ownership note

The National Pension Service held 8.42% as of its March 17, 2026 filing. CEO Score Daily reported that the stake has since reached double digits, but the exact figure is unconfirmed. This is not investment advice.
