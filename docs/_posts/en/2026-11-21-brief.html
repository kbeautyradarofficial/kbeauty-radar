1. US$11.1bn in nine months, and Europe is growing fastest

Korean cosmetics exports reached US$11.1bn (provisional) in January–September 2026, up 31.1% year on year. That is a record for the first three quarters. It is also close to the 2025 full-year total of US$11.4bn (CEO Score Daily, citing MFDS). Trade ministry customs data reportedly puts September alone at US$1.51bn (+31.4%), the first month above US$1.5bn (KDI EIEC).

  • Largest market: US, US$2.35bn, 21.1% of the total (Jan–Sep)
  • Fastest growth: Netherlands +292.6%, UK +179.3% (Jan–Sep)

For EU buyers: the Netherlands is a logistics gateway, so most of this growth is product heading into the wider EU. Before you commit to volume, ask Korean suppliers to show their CPNP notifications and name their Responsible Person. Missing compliance paperwork is the most common reason launches slip.

2. COSRX goes mass in Australia with about 2,000 doors

From mid-November, COSRX adds Kmart and Target, which takes it to about 2,000 doors in Australia. It is already stocked at Chemist Warehouse (~350 stores), Coles (850+ stores, since Sept 25) and BIG W. The lead SKU is The 6 Peptide Skin Booster Serum, which was No. 1 in Olive Young's skincare category as of July 1, 2026 (Seoul Shinmun).

Coway's brand Re:NK, run by its subsidiary Hillarybe, reportedly entered 50 US Costco stores in October (Edaily). A reported rollout to about 850 Daiso stores has not been confirmed.

For grocery, discount and club retailers: K-beauty brands are now building a mass-retail playbook. Negotiate pack sizes and price points just for your channel, so shoppers can't compare your shelf price with Amazon or pharmacy pricing.

3. Olive Young adds five California stores

Olive Young reportedly plans five more California stores by the end of 1H 2027. They include Irvine Spectrum Center, Del Amo Fashion Center, Bloom on Third and Brea Mall. It already has two US stores: Pasadena (opened May 29, 2026) and Westfield Century City (opened June 13, 2026) (Cosmetics Business / Korea JoongAng Daily).

For US specialty retailers: a seven-store Olive Young footprint will chase the same hero SKUs you carry. Lock in exclusives or early-launch windows with brands now.

4. ODM capacity is the bottleneck

Cosmax's Korean entity reportedly produced an average of 69.3M units a month in January–August 2026. That is about 2.5x its 2021 level of 27.43M. Group capacity is expected to reach about 4bn units a year, up from about 3.5bn, once the Pyeongtaek Plant 1 expansion is finished (Sidae, Economist Korea).

For buyers: smaller brands that share ODM lines may quote longer lead times. Confirm Q1 2027 purchase orders and delivery dates before year-end.

Also: Korea–China FTA follow-up

Korea and China reportedly agreed the substance of their follow-up talks on services and investment on Nov 18 in Shenzhen, 8 years and 8 months after the talks began. Korean films and games get most-favoured-nation treatment. We found no provision on cosmetics tariffs (Korean press).
