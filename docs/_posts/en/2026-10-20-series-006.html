Bottom line

Southeast Asia is turning from a channel race into a compliance race. Thai shelves are already crowded with K-beauty. Indonesia has made halal a de facto condition of entry. Vietnam is still at the buyer-matching stage.

Note: articles from this window (mid-September to mid-October 2026) did not include fresh country-level export values or market share. Check official trade data separately.

Channels: Olive Young inside 48 Sephora stores

  • CJ Olive Young placed its 'K-Beauty Edit' curation in 48 Sephora stores across Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore and Hong Kong (reported October 12, 2026). Hankyoreh
  • A K-Beauty Edit pop-up opened in Singapore alongside the rollout. Korea Report
  • It follows the US Sephora rollout. For smaller brands, Olive Young curation becomes a route onto Sephora shelves without a direct vendor deal.

The door count matters less than who makes the curation. Korean Olive Young sell-out and review data will likely act as the entry ticket.

Regulation: Indonesia checks the warehouse too

  • Ahead of mandatory halal labeling, enforcement is extending to logistics and storage, with K-food and K-beauty flagged for targeted checks. Seoul Focus
  • Halal labeling was the main talking point at Cosmobeaute Indonesia 2026. CNC NEWS
  • Korea's MFDS expanded halal certification cooperation with Indonesia in September. Etoday
  • The same month, the world's first cosmetics regulators' council launched with Korea as chair. Food Today

A product certificate alone won't cover you. Whether the distributor and 3PL warehouse run a halal control system is half the risk.

Country notes

  • Thailand: Newsis reports K-beauty dominating Bangkok shelves and looks at the other side of that story. Newsis L&C Bio is growing its skin booster 'Re2O' there. Edaily IBITA plans to expand in Thailand in 2027. Beautynury
  • Indonesia: Laneige teamed up with Hyundai Motor Indonesia for the first time at its 3rd 'Hyundai Sunday'. Haninpost Seoul's Gangnam district logged $810,000 in consultations there for K-medical and K-beauty. Korea Post
  • Vietnam: 17 Korean cosmetics companies joined a KHIDI market program. Medical Observer 16 Daejeon companies signed 13 MOUs with buyers in Ho Chi Minh City. Pressian

Checklist for buyers and exporters

  • Indonesia: audit halal coverage from product to distributor to 3PL warehouse; write responsibility into contracts
  • Use local certification support programs, such as Yongin's overseas certification aid for cosmetics exporters
  • Thailand: assume shelf saturation; back one or two differentiated hero SKUs
  • Sephora curation: prepare Korean Olive Young sell-out and review data
  • Vietnam: fast sample, quote and MOQ replies after buyer meetings decide reorders
