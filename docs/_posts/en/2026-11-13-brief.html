1. Cosmax tops ₩800bn in a quarter for the first time

According to Pinpoint News, citing a November 11 filing, cosmetics ODM Cosmax reported consolidated Q3 2026 revenue of ₩875.8bn, up 49.6% year on year. It's the first quarter above ₩800bn, and it came in a seasonally slow quarter. Operating profit was reported at ₩83.3bn, up 94.9%.

For context, Q3 2025 was ₩585.6bn in revenue and ₩42.7bn in operating profit, with profit down 1.6% that year (Byline Network).

For buyers: Cosmax's order book is running hot. If you plan a Q1 2027 launch or a larger reorder, confirm with your brand partners that production slots are booked. Lead times are the risk here, not demand.

2. Aekyung: best Q3 sales ever, operating loss anyway

Aekyung Industrial posted Q3 2026 revenue of ₩190.4bn, its highest Q3 on record and up about 12.5% (Sisa Economy). It still swung to a ₩3.3bn operating loss, against ₩7.3bn profit a year earlier. The company blamed higher marketing investment (SBS Biz). Reports put cosmetics revenue at ₩58.7bn (+13.9%) and household goods at ₩127.8bn (+11.5%) (The Value News and others).

Put the two results side by side and you can see where the margin went. The manufacturer grew profit on volume. The brand bought growth with ad spend. When a brand partner asks for co-op marketing money or deeper promo support, expect them to push harder. Their P&L needs it.

3. Beauty of Joseon parent targets a ₩10tn IPO

Goodai Global, which owns Beauty of Joseon, is reportedly preparing an IPO at a target valuation of ₩10tn. Its August 2025 convertible bond issue of ₩800bn valued it at about ₩4.4tn (Digital Daily). FY2025 consolidated net income was reported at ₩233.7bn, implying roughly 48x earnings at ₩10tn (The Fact). Mirae Asset Securities is reported as lead manager, with NH Investment & Securities, Citigroup Global Markets and Morgan Stanley as co-managers. CEO Chun Joo-hyuk reportedly holds 96.5% (The Fact).

In the US, Guinness Atkinson filed for a NYSE-listed K-beauty ETF covering four segments: brands, ODMs and suppliers, distributors, and aesthetics/derma. It was awaiting approval as of the July 2026 report, with no fund name or ticker disclosed (Personal Care Insights).

This isn't investment advice. The practical point: a brand heading toward a listing often tightens pricing, distribution and channel discipline. If you distribute a brand in that position, expect exclusivity and MAP terms to come up for review.

4. H1 exports: $7.0bn, US +42.1%, three European markets up 70%+

Per MFDS data cited by Personal Care Insights, Korea's cosmetics exports hit a half-year record of $7.0bn in H1 2026. Exports to the US were $1.45bn, up 42.1%, the largest single market. The UK, the Netherlands and Poland each grew more than 70%.

These are H1 figures, so the data is a few months old. Still, Europe is clearly the next growth leg. EU and UK retailers should check that each SKU has CPNP/SCPN notification and a named Responsible Person before the PO, not after.

5. Korea passes last year's tourist record by October 27

Foreign arrivals to Korea passed 2025's full-year record of 18.94 million on October 27, 2026. The government target is 23 million (The Star). H1 alone topped 10 million (The Korea Times).

More of your shoppers get to try K-beauty in Seoul before they buy it at home. Ask brands for their in-store sell-out by visitor nationality. It gives you an early read on which SKUs will move in your market.
