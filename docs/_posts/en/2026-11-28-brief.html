1. APR buys 87.3% of Dr.Melaxin's parent for about ₩390bn

APR, the company behind Medicube, said in a 26 Nov filing that it will buy 87.3% of Brand501, which runs the derma brand Dr.Melaxin, for about ₩390bn. Closing is set for 31 Dec 2026. About ₩320bn goes to existing shares (74.35%) and about ₩70bn to newly issued shares (12.95%). (Aju Business Daily)

Dr.Melaxin was the #2 beauty brand by sales on US TikTok Shop in H1 2026, according to Charm.io. APR's revenue for 1–3Q 2026 passed ₩2tn, already more than its full-year 2025 revenue. (Aju Business Daily) The stated aim is to cut single-brand risk. Reports put Medicube at more than 90% of APR's 2025 revenue: about ₩1.4tn of ₩1.5273tn. (NewsSpace)

  • For buyers: once the deal closes, one Korean group will own the reported #1 Amazon Beauty product of Black Friday 2025 (Medicube Zero Pore Pad) and TikTok Shop's H1 2026 #2 beauty brand. Expect joint talks on terms, promo calendars and shelf space across both lines in 2027.

2. Cost structure, not sales, sets the value

Reports say Brand501 posted ₩477.2bn in revenue and ₩41.8bn in operating profit for 1–3Q 2026, a margin of about 8.8%. SK Securities estimates its SG&A at 77.1% of revenue, against 54.0% at APR. An analyst note calls the price cheap on sales but expensive on earnings. For comparison, the COSRX and Serin Company deals were done at 2.7–3.2x PSR and 10–11x PER. (NewsSpace)

  • A brand built on TikTok Shop carries heavy creator and ad costs. If APR cuts them, expect tighter promo spend and possibly new trade terms for Dr.Melaxin in offline and marketplace accounts.

3. Black Friday #1–3 on Amazon: reported, not yet confirmed

Korean outlet MyDaily reports that APR and Beauty Selection held Amazon's #1–3 spots in Western-market Black Friday. The category, ranking time and products have not been confirmed. (MyDaily)

Last year's baseline: during Black Friday 2025 (20 Nov–1 Dec), Medicube's Zero Pore Pad was reportedly #1 in Amazon Beauty, with Medicube also at #2 and #3. US sales of Medicube and April Skin across all channels more than tripled year on year. (Digital Daily) At Prime Day in July 2026, Medicube was reportedly the #1 search term across all categories and placed up to 11 products in the Beauty & Personal Care top 100. (New Daily)

4. October exports: $1.44bn, +57.5%, but below September

Korea's cosmetics exports were $1.44bn in October 2026, up 57.5% year on year (MOTIR customs data, preliminary). That is the best October on record (the previous high was $1.03bn in October 2024), though below September's all-time monthly high of $1.51bn. (Sateconomy)

  • Our read: part of the Q4 surge is retailers stocking up for BFCM. November and December data will show whether that stock actually sold. Check your own sell-through before you lock in Q1 orders.

5. Korea holds more than 26% of US cosmetics imports

US imports of Korean cosmetics (HS 3304) were $1,161.82m in January–July 2026, up 11.4%, and Korea's share was above 26% (US Commerce data via KOTRA New York). Exports to Belgium were $21.335m in January–August, up 35.5%. (Sateconomy) Reports also put Korea's H1 2026 cosmetics exports at a record $7.0bn (+27.3%). Hair-product exports were $232.72m in January–May (up more than 30%), and the industry expects the full year to pass $500m for the first time. (Newsis, Asia Economy)
