1. Medicube's UK path: 1,300+ Boots doors first, then a Selfridges counter

APR's Medicube has opened its first permanent counter in Europe at Selfridges' Oxford Street flagship. That moves the brand up from UK drugstores into department stores (News1).

  • Medicube is sold in more than 1,300 Boots stores. K-beauty sales at Boots grew by triple digits, and Medicube search volume rose about 20%. The comparison period was not given (News1, reported 22 Nov 2026).
  • Reports say the brand went into John Lewis via Skin Cupid in April (one of 20 K-beauty brands), finished its Space NK rollout in August, and is entering 150 M&S stores (Korea Credit News).
  • APR plans to add more major UK retail channels in 2027 (News1).

For UK buyers, the order of steps is what to learn from. Specialty and drugstore doors proved sell-through and built search demand. The department-store counter came only after that. When a Korean brand pitches you, ask for the same evidence: sell-through by door and search trends, not just its rankings in Korea.

2. Olive Young's first color-cosmetics store, in a district where 84% of sales are to foreign shoppers

Olive Young reportedly opened "Color Ground Hongdae" on 20 Nov. It has 4 floors and 174 pyeong of selling space, and 26% of its brands are new to Olive Young (BizWatch).

  • Why Hongdae: in Jan–Oct 2026, customers in their teens and 20s made up more than half of sales at Olive Young's Hongdae-area stores, and foreign customers made up 84%.

This is a live test of Korean makeup on international shoppers. If you are scouting K-color brands beyond the usual skincare names, watch which of the new brands here gain traction. They are the likely candidates for overseas listings next.

3. Korea passes US$1 trillion in exports; cosmetics lean on the US

Korea's 2026 exports passed US$1 trillion on 19 Nov (preliminary), making it the 4th country to do so after Germany, the US and China (Korea.kr). The government says cosmetics, the top export item for SMEs, beat their previous full-year record within Jan–Oct, driven by North America, Europe and Latin America. It gave no dollar amount.

  • H1 2026 cosmetics exports were reportedly US$7.0bn (MFDS, provisional), up 27.3% YoY (CosinKorea).
  • Jan–Apr exports: US$880m to the US (+40.5%) vs US$650m to China (-14.0%), per SBS Biz.

Demand is shifting toward Western markets, so supplier pricing will react to tariff and FX moves faster than before. Lock in pricing terms and lead times for Q1 orders now, and ask suppliers how much of their capacity is committed to US accounts.

4. Amorepacific measures Amazon events on new customers

Amorepacific reportedly grew sales 201% YoY in Amazon's Big Spring Sale (7 days from 25 Mar), and called new-customer growth the most encouraging result. Reports say its Black Friday push has the same goal (Hankyung). Prime Day (23–26 Jun) sales were up 20% in the US and 22% in Europe (Amorepacific newsroom). For reference, its 2024 BFCM results were Laneige +127%, Sulwhasoo +308% and Mise-en-scène +1,092% (Amorepacific newsroom).

Amazon is also promoting viral Korean skincare at up to 46% off this Black Friday (Yahoo headline). There is no data yet on K-beauty's share of event sales. If you run your own holiday promotions, judge them on new-to-brand customers and repeat purchases. A sales spike at deep discounts doesn't tell you much on its own.
