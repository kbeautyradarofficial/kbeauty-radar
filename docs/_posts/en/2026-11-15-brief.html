1. Beauty Kurly grew 25%, slower than the platform overall

Kurly posted Q3 2026 GMV of KRW 1.1496 trillion, up 32.1% year on year. Consolidated revenue hit a quarterly record of KRW 777.9 billion (+34.4%), and operating profit was KRW 10 billion (+63.5%), its seventh profitable quarter in a row. (Dealsite)

Beauty Kurly GMV rose 25% over the same period, against 32.8% for Market Kurly, its grocery side. Dealsite credits new brand additions and demand for functional and dermo-cosmetic products.

Kurly has reportedly said its 2025 Beauty Festa drew about 16,000 visitors and lifted participating partners' GMV an average of 9x (Dealsite). This year's edition is reportedly a four-day event at DDP in Seoul with 45 curated brands (Korean Center News).

  • For buyers: Kurly's beauty curation leans dermo and functional. If you're scouting brands building traction in Korea, the Festa brand list is a ready-made shortlist.

2. LYKO sources in Seoul. One listing covers 8 markets

According to a KOTRA notice, KOTRA hosted 1:1 sourcing meetings with LYKO at its Seoul headquarters on 12 November. KOTRA describes LYKO as the Nordics' largest omnichannel beauty retailer. Categories sought: skincare, makeup, body and hair care, and inner beauty. (KOTRA notice)

The precedent: Amorepacific's Mamonde reportedly used LYKO to launch in 8 countries at once in October 2025, including Sweden, Norway and Finland. (Amorepacific newsroom)

  • For European retailers: expect more Korean indie brands arriving with Nordic experience. Before you list them, check CPNP notification, the PIF, an EU Responsible Person and local-language labeling.

3. Cosmax KRW 794.9bn: your private label and your K-brands may share a factory

Q2 2026 global cosmetics ODM sales were reported as Cosmax KRW 794.9 billion, Kolmar Korea KRW 649.7 billion and Intercos KRW 465.8 billion. (Money Today)

Edaily reports Korean ODMs are taking more orders from Chinese brands selling 'Made in Korea' products. Analysts expect strong order pipelines through Q3–Q4 2026 as global retailers build inventory ahead of major retail events. (Edaily)

  • For buyers: 'Made in Korea' on the label no longer tells you who owns the brand or the formula. Ask for the brand's country of ownership and whether the formula is exclusive.
  • Peak-season capacity is tight. Lock Q1 reorder quantities early.

4. Korean SME cosmetics exports: USD 2.79bn in Q3

Korean SME cosmetics exports reportedly reached a record USD 2.79 billion in Q3 2026. The primary source isn't confirmed, and the figure looks like trade-statistics data rather than Korea's MFDS production figures. (Outlook Respawn)

  • For buyers: when a supplier cites Korean export numbers, ask which statistical basis and period they're using before you compare decks.
