1. Korea Kolmar logs ₩6.8B in Melbourne talks. None of it is signed yet

ODM Korea Kolmar says it held about 150 export meetings at the 2026 Melbourne Korea Expo (Nov 5-7), worth an estimated ₩6.8 billion. The company says that was more than 10% of all meetings at the fair (Asia Economy). That figure measures meetings, not contracts.

Kolmar brought six of its client brands: Round Lab, Mixsoon, Herblum, iUNIK, Ninetails and B.WANTS. By the company's own estimate, about 30,000 consumers came to the two public days and about 15,000 stopped at its booth.

The bigger number is the market itself. Citing KOTRA, the article says Australia's imports of Korean basic skincare reached about US$147.7M in 2025, up 42.1% year on year. Reports say the fair had about 110 participating companies (Etoday).

  • For Australian buyers: an ODM-led booth puts several client brands from one manufacturer in a single meeting. Ask about MOQs and lead times for each brand.

2. Kurly's private label goes to Amazon US three months after launch

Dew:Era, the first beauty private label from Korean e-grocer Kurly, launched in September 2026 and plans to list on Amazon and TikTok Shop in the US before year-end. It is starting with mask packs (Seoul Economic Daily).

The range has just two SKUs, both two-step film masks: one for brightening and one for hydration. Kurly will run the listings alongside Kurly USA, the cross-border store it opened in August 2025. Kurly's Q2 2026 revenue was ₩734.8B (+27%) and operating profit was ₩27.4B, its best quarter on record. The industry reads the US move as a way to lift its valuation before another IPO attempt.

Reports say Amazon puts the growth in Korean sellers' beauty sales for Jan-Sep 2026 at double the year-earlier pace (Kukmin Ilbo).

  • For US retailers: Korean retailer-owned labels are now going straight to marketplaces. Expect low-SKU, mask-led tests, and judge them on review velocity before you commit shelf space.

3. Shinsegae's Hyperground: fashion in Taipei, fashion plus beauty in Kuala Lumpur

Shinsegae is opening its first Hyperground pop-ups in Taiwan and Malaysia (Asia Economy):

  • Taipei, Breeze Center (1F luxury hall), until Feb 2027 (about 4 months): 6 women's contemporary fashion brands on rotation, starting with Lecto.
  • Kuala Lumpur, Seibu at The Exchange TRX (1F), until Mar 2027 (about 5 months): about 10 fashion and beauty brands. The first beauty round is Headspa7, Fivevibe and Dinto.

Reports say the New York edition at Hudson Yards runs Oct 1-Dec 31 with about 30 K-beauty brands (Seoul Economic Daily), and Shinsegae plans about 10 pop-ups a year across Japan, Thailand, the US, Taiwan and Malaysia (News1).

  • For ASEAN distributors: rotating pop-ups test demand for you. Track which brands sell through in KL and approach them about permanent distribution before the run ends in March.
