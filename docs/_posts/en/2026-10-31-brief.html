1. September cosmetics exports: US$1.506bn, the first month above US$1.5bn

Korea exported US$1.506bn of cosmetics in September 2026, up 31.4% YoY, according to the trade ministry's all-company data. The previous monthly high was US$1.352bn in April 2026. (Cosmorning)

Jan–Sep totals US$11.139bn (+31.2%). That's about 98% of 2025's full-year record of roughly US$11.4bn. The remaining gap is under US$300m, so the October numbers will very likely push 2026 past last year's total.

Cosmorning also estimates September color cosmetics exports at about US$230m (+132.6%). The figure isn't final, so read it as a direction: the export mix is widening beyond skincare.

  • For buyers: if Q4 reorders pile up, lead times tend to stretch. Place Q1 2027 orders early, especially for color SKUs.

2. SME cosmetics exports: US$2.79bn in Q3 (+28.6%), still behind the total

According to the Ministry of SMEs and Startups, small and mid-sized firms exported US$2.79bn of cosmetics in Q3 2026. That's up 28.6% and a Q3 record. Cosmetics and semiconductors led SME export growth, and total SME exports reached US$33.4bn (+8.8%). (Korea.kr)

For Jan–Sep, SME cosmetics exports reached US$7.65bn (+26.4%), while total cosmetics exports grew 31.2%. The two ministries count differently, so treat this as a direction, not a precise gap. Even so, larger brands and ODM-made volume appear to be growing faster than indie SMEs. The number of exporting SMEs rose only 1.6% to 90,765 (preliminary). Most of the growth looks like existing exporters shipping more, not new entrants.

  • For buyers: when you vet a new indie brand, ask which ODM makes it and how much 2027 production capacity it has secured.

3. SME cosmetics to the US: +14.5%, half the overall rate

SME cosmetics exports to the US were US$560m in Q3 2026, up 14.5%. That's about half the overall SME cosmetics growth rate of 28.6%. Across all products, the US has overtaken China as Korean SMEs' largest export market. (Korea.kr)

A correction: the "Europe +58.6%, North America +33.1%" figures in some coverage are all-product SME export growth rates, not cosmetics. The official release doesn't support reading them as beauty figures.

  • For retailers and distributors: the US is still a very large market, but its growth rate is lagging. If that continues, expect more Korean brands to seek non-US distribution. Non-US partners can use that when negotiating terms.

4. Musinsa Beauty's first standalone store: ₩250m in three days

Musinsa Beauty opened its first standalone store near Hongdae, Seoul, on Sep 11. It covers about 400 pyeong across four floors (B1–3F) and carries 870 brands and about 12,000 SKUs. First-three-day sales were ₩250m. A 400-pyeong store in Seongsu will be the second location. (MTN)

Hankyung reports that the beauty zone at Musinsa Megastore Seongsu, open since April, grew transaction value by about 20% through August. (Hankyung) Musinsa posted record H1 revenue of ₩821.7bn, but operating profit fell because of global investment. (MTN)

  • For buyers: on Seoul scouting trips, visit Musinsa Beauty as well as Olive Young. It gives you a second view of which indie brands young shoppers buy in stores.
