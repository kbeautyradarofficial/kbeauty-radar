Bottom line: As of October 2026, APR (Medicube) is running three engines at once: a skincare hero SKU, Amazon tentpole events, and a new aesthetics line built on skin boosters. The market has stopped asking how much it sells and started asking how much it keeps.

1. Hero SKU: EGF NAD ampoule hits 1M units in 8 months

Per Star Daily News, the Medicube EGF NAD firming ampoule crossed 1 million cumulative units eight months after launch (reported Oct 16). The actives sit right in the product name, so the name doubles as a search keyword. That pays off directly in listings and product pages.

2. Record Amazon Prime Big Deal Days

JoongAng Ilbo reports Medicube posted its highest-ever sales during October's Prime Big Deal Days. Economic Review puts Medicube alongside Laneige as K-beauty names carrying Amazon momentum into Black Friday. October reviews and rank are the starting line for November.

3. Two skin boosters, Chile launch in November

CosinKorea says two PN skin boosters made their first shipment to Chile, and Yonhap puts the official launch in November. Chosunbiz frames it as an expansion into medical devices, and Global Economic calls aesthetics a new revenue stream. The play: carry a skincare brand name into the treatment category, with Latin America, not the US, as the first test market.

4. AI consultation on the brand site

Gonggam News reports Medicube added an AI advisor to its official store that recommends products by skin concern. MediaPen describes APR rebuilding its export model around beauty tech. As SKU counts grow, conversion goes to whoever tells shoppers fastest what to buy.

5. The numbers: Q3 operating profit forecast and margin

Per Vegan News, KB Securities forecasts APR's Q3 2026 operating profit at KRW 169.5B (reported Oct 17). Asia Economy says annual revenue is approaching KRW 3 trillion. On the other side, Bloter (Oct 15) covered the stock's steepest drop since listing and noted margins slipped even as revenue doubled. A day later, News1 reported a 10% rebound amid views that currency fears were overdone. For operators: as overseas sales scale, margin structure and FX exposure decide the scorecard.

What buyers and retailers should watch

  • Hero SKUs with the active in the name move fastest in search-led channels
  • October Amazon sell-through is your signal for Black Friday inventory depth
  • Skincare brands are stretching into aesthetics; check device classification and clinic channels before taking extensions
  • Margin pressure at fast-growing brands can reshape pricing and promo terms; lock Q4 terms early
