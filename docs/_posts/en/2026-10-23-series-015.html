Bottom line: Europe is now ahead of North America as a destination for Korean cosmetics, and the UK is the fastest mover inside it. The numbers vary by source, so always quote the agency and period with them.

Europe vs North America: Europe leads on every count

  • Korea Customs Service, 58 European countries, H1 2026: Europe US$1.596bn vs North America (US and Canada) US$1.570bn. First time Europe has led on a half-year basis. (Seoul Economic Daily)
  • Hankyung's own analysis, H1 2026: Europe US$1.525bn vs North America US$1.491bn, a US$33.8m gap. Share of total exports: Europe 22.9%, North America 22.4%. Asia stays first at US$3.143bn. Total Korean cosmetics exports hit about US$7bn in H1, a record. (Hankyung)
  • KITA, Jan–Jul 2026: Europe US$1.611bn, up 65% from US$975.49m a year earlier and already 94% of the full-year 2025 total of US$1.713bn. North America: US$1.470bn, up 39%. (Financial News)
  • Monthly, Europe has led since February. The gap grew from US$2.06m in February to US$33.44m in July (KITA). Yonhap Infomax reports the lead held in the latest month; exact figures are not confirmed.

Why the numbers don't match

Seoul Economic Daily and Hankyung used the same customs data but land about US$70m apart for H1. The difference is which countries count as Europe. KITA covers a different period. For internal reports and buyer decks, state the agency, the regional definition and the period. Pick one source for annual KPIs and stick with it.

Value per kg +46%: Europe pays more

In H1 2026, export value per kg to Europe was 46% higher than to North America, while North America still leads clearly on volume (Hankyung). Volume sits in North America; value sits in Europe. When value per kg is high, freight is a smaller share of revenue, so air freight to shorten lead times may pencil out better for Europe than for the US. Rerun your landed-cost sheet for European SKUs.

UK: plan it separately from the EU

UK exports reached US$262.75m in Jan–Jul, up 162% from US$100.43m and already above the 2025 full-year total of US$187.34m. The UK moved from 11th to 5th among K-beauty destinations (Financial News, KITA data).

TikTok Shop is a major driver. Per a TikTok announcement reported by Dong-A Ilbo and Chosun Biz, K-beauty sellers on TikTok Shop UK grew 875% in the year to September, and a K-beauty product sells every 5 seconds. TikTok also ran its first K-beauty-only offline event, 'Where Glow Begins', in London. Money Today reports Beauty of Joseon's average daily sales roughly quadrupled during September's K-beauty Super Brand Week. Offline, Newstap reports KISS is in about 450 Boots stores.

Roughly nine times the sellers means roughly nine times the competition. A listing alone no longer sells; campaign slots and livestream calendars decide who wins.

EU offline: vendors over direct entry

APR targets KRW 500bn in European sales this year. It went direct into US retail but uses distribution vendors such as Silicon2 for European offline (Daily Hankook). Hankyung says skincare and indie brands are leading growth and landing in Sephora and Douglas. In France, OhmyNews reports exports up 71.5% alongside tougher pharmacy competition; the period is unconfirmed.

Checklist for exporters and buyers

  • One data source for KPIs, always with the period
  • Separate UK (UK RP, SCPN) and EU (EU RP, CPNP) registration tracks
  • TikTok Shop UK: book Brand Week and livestream slots with one hero SKU; land stock before the campaign
  • EU vendor deals: put country exclusivity, margin structure and reorder terms in writing
  • Model air vs sea freight separately for high-value European SKUs
  • Q4 gift sets and advent calendars: recheck EU labelling and packaging before locking promotions
