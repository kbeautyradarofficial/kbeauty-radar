Bottom line

The US is now carrying K-beauty's export growth. Exports to the US rose 40.6% in January–September 2026, while exports to China grew 0.4%. Revenue is at a record. But a strong won and rising US non-tariff barriers are eating into margins. Before holiday orders, look at landed margin first and growth rates second.

Exports: $11.1B in Jan–Sep, 21.1% to the US

  • Korea's cosmetics exports reached $11.1B in January–September 2026, up 31.1% (MFDS preliminary data). That's close to last year's full-year record of $11.4B. (SBS Biz)
  • The US was the #1 destination at $2.35B (+40.6%), or 21.1% of the total. China followed with $1.58B and Japan with $0.89B. (Korea Post)
  • Exports to China grew only 0.4%. China's share of Korean cosmetics exports was 46.7% in 2022 and is now around 14%. (The K Beauty Science)
  • Q3 exports were $4.17B, up 7.5% from Q2's $3.88B. (SBS Biz)
  • September exports alone were $1.51B (+31.4%), the highest monthly total on record. (CosinKorea)

A note on data. Beauty Economy used Korea Customs product codes and found 14% growth in US exports (reported Oct 18). That series covers a different scope than MFDS's +40.6%. Pick one series and use it every time. (Beauty Economy)

US imports: flat market, rising Korean share

Electronic Times reports that Korea is pulling further ahead of competing countries in US cosmetics imports. Beautynury finds the same thing: total US imports are flat while K-beauty's share grows. Korea isn't growing because the market is bigger. It's taking share from countries like France. (Electronic Times, Beautynury)

Channels: one brand leads TikTok Shop, K-beauty grows on Amazon

  • TikTok Shop: Industry News reports that APR's Medicube accounts for 19% of US TikTok Shop skincare sales (Oct 12). (Industry News)
  • Amazon: Beautynury found that Medicube slowed, yet the combined share of K-beauty brands still grew (Oct 12). The demand is for the category, not just one brand. (Beautynury)

Our take: the channel question is settled. The risk now is concentration in a few top brands. Eugene Investment & Securities says K-beauty competition has moved from market share to share of wallet (Financial Post Korea). For retailers, that means deciding how many steps of a shopper's routine a Korean brand can cover, not just which brand is hot.

Categories: skincare up, makeup down

Color cosmetics exports fell 3.6% to $1.11B in January–September (MFDS via SBS Biz), even as total exports grew 31%. Skincare is driving the growth (KMJ). Some brands are moving beyond skincare. LG H&H's Dr.Groot won its first Allure Best of Beauty award, a test of Korean scalp care in North America (NewsValue).

Two risks

  • Currency: Chosunbiz reports that a strong won is squeezing K-beauty profits despite record exports. Expect Korean vendors to push back on price or promotion depth. (Chosunbiz)
  • Regulation: Cosmorning ranks the US first among K-beauty export markets for rising non-tariff barriers. (Cosmorning)

Checklist for buyers and retailers

  • Lock holiday reorders and delivery dates now, based on recent promotion sell-through
  • Give space to proven skincare hero SKUs; order Korean makeup in test quantities
  • Before adding shelf space for a TikTok Shop leader, compare its online share with your own sell-through
  • Agree on holiday cost prices and promotion depth with Korean vendors before the currency moves again
  • Confirm MoCRA facility registration, product listing and labeling files with every Korean supplier
