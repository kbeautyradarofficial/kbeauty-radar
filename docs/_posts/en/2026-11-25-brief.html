1. Cosmetics are 47.6% of Korea's consumer goods exports

Korea Customs Service data, compiled by Yonhap and reported by The Korea Herald: from Jan 1 to Nov 19, 2026, consumer goods exports reached $28.65bn, up 21.5% year on year. That already beats the full-year 2025 total of $27.02bn.

Cosmetics did most of the work. They reached $13.65bn (+34.6%), or 47.6% of consumer goods exports. For full-year 2025 the share was 43% ($11.61bn).

  • Same period, same source: food was $10.94bn (+9.3%), fashion $3.13bn (+14%) and cultural goods such as albums and books $920m (+36.5%).
  • For sourcing: Korean beauty suppliers are growing 13 points faster than consumer goods overall. Confirm capacity and lead times with your brands and their ODMs early for 2027 programs.

Data note: Korea runs two export series. Customs puts 2025 cosmetics at $11.61bn. The MFDS/trade ministry cosmetics statistics reportedly put it at $11.4bn (Newsis). When a brand quotes "record exports," ask which series and which period.

2. The US passes China, for consumer goods overall

From Jan 1 to Nov 19, the US bought $5.69bn of Korean consumer goods. China bought $5.1bn and Japan $2.9bn. It is the first time in 22 years that the US has led (The Korea Herald). These are totals for all consumer goods, not a cosmetics-only ranking.

  • With beauty at 47.6% of that mix, the center of gravity is moving to the US. Expect more Korean brands pitching US retail.
  • Ask each brand how its US sales split between Amazon/DTC and brick-and-mortar. That split shows how much in-store sell-through it has actually proven.

3. China is growing again, through different brands

A Kyobo Securities report cited by Dealsite puts cosmetics exports to China at $190m for Sep 1–20, 2026 (preliminary). That is up 76% year on year and 193% on the same period a month earlier. Kyobo expected September to pass $200m for the first time in about two years.

  • Anua returned to China via Sephora China in August 2026, after leaving in 2023.
  • d'Alba Global's Greater China sales rose 130% to ₩20bn in 2025, and it was in 91 offline stores in China by the end of Q4.
  • Medicube named a Shanghai distributor as its China strategic partner in July 2026.

This growth isn't coming from the old daigou and duty-free trade. Brands proven in global markets are going in through formal retail and distribution. For buyers, the US-first, China-second pattern is a useful filter: these brands have already cleared formal retail in more than one major market.

4. Expo "consultation" figures are not orders

According to Hankyung, Hyundai Home Shopping's Koasis export platform reported about $7.94m (₩12.2bn) in export consultations with 15 brands at the July 2026 Hanoi Hallyu Expo. It reports cumulative consultations of ₩99.3bn across 144 partners in 7 countries.

Consultation values are not contracted exports. Judge supplier traction by POs, reorders and sell-through, not headline expo numbers.
