1. APR Q3: revenue doubled, margin down 4 points

APR, the company behind Medicube, reported preliminary consolidated Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 773.1bn, up 100.3% YoY. Operating profit was KRW 160.6bn (+68.9%), an operating margin of about 20.8% (재외동포신문, citing the Oct 30 disclosure).

Q3 overseas revenue was KRW 722.9bn (+135%), 94% of the total. North America made up 46% of Q3 revenue and Europe 23%. Jan–Sep 2026 revenue of KRW 2.134tn and operating profit of KRW 503.5bn already exceed APR's full-year 2025 results.

Operating profit came in 7.8% below the Yonhap Infomax consensus of KRW 174.2bn. Some brokers used a KRW 181.4bn consensus, so the size of the miss depends on the benchmark.

SBS Biz reports the cause was a stronger won. The exchange rate dropped sharply at quarter-end, creating FX losses on foreign-currency receivables, booked as other operating expenses. Operating margin fell from 24.8% in Q2 to 20.8% in Q3.

  • For buyers: Korean suppliers that earn mostly in USD or EUR are now watching FX closely. Expect invoice currency, payment terms and price reviews to come up in 2027 negotiations.
  • If you have room on payment timing, use it. Faster payment is worth more to a supplier when the won is volatile.

2. From Lookfantastic to Selfridges: Biodance and Medicube go permanent

Selfridges has opened 'The Beauty Workshop', a K-beauty area in the Ground Floor Beauty Hall of its Oxford Street flagship. Biodance opened its first permanent retail counter anywhere in the world there, distributed by Luneia Brands. Medicube opened its first permanent counter in Europe in the same space (TheIndustry.beauty, Nov 2).

Both brands proved themselves online first. As reported in November, UK Lookfantastic sales of Biodance's Bio-Collagen Real Deep Mask rose 331.5% YoY, and Medicube's Zero Pore Pad 2.0 rose 919.9%. Biodance has sold more than 350 million hydrogel masks worldwide; Medicube's AGE-R devices have passed 6 million units.

The area also stocks Beauty of Joseon, SKIN1004, Axis-Y, Thank You Farmer, VT Cosmetics and Dr. Reju-All. FashionNetwork UK reports that Biodance prices on selfridges.com start at £13.90.

  • For retailers: online sell-through is now the main reason K-brands win department-store space. Ask brands for SKU-level e-commerce sell-through before you discuss a counter.
  • The distributor-run counter (Biodance with Luneia Brands) is a model you can repeat with brands that have no local team yet.

3. Schwan Cosmetics plans a Seoul innovation hub

According to a Cision press release and Cosmetics Business, white-label colour cosmetics maker Schwan Cosmetics will open a North Asia Innovation Hub in Seoul in January 2027. The aim is to bring Korean skincare, active-ingredient and longevity research into makeup faster. Schwan already has an Asia sales office in Shanghai and operations in Tianjin. It is reported to present the hub at Cosmopack Asia in Hong Kong (Nov 10–12, Hall 3, Booth 3-D10).

  • For private-label buyers: Korean-style skincare actives in colour cosmetics may soon be available from a Western OEM you already use. Ask about it at Cosmopack.
