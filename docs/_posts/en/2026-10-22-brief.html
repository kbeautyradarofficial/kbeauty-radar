1. Cosmax shows up in L'Oréal's earnings release

L'Oréal posted Q3 2026 (Jul–Sep) sales of €11,561.4m, up 7.2% like-for-like (+11.9% reported). Nine-month sales were €35,337.6m, up 6.9% LFL. North Asia grew 7.8% LFL in Q3 (+5.5% over 9M), and the company said a rebound in travel drove its growth in Korea. (L'Oréal Finance)

The same release says L'Oréal signed an MoU with Korean ODM Cosmax to work on next-generation beauty innovation. (L'Oréal Finance)

  • For buyers, this means Korean ODM formulation is now going into L'Oréal's own R&D pipeline, not just into indie brands.
  • If your private label or exclusive brands come from a large Korean ODM, check the contracts for formula exclusivity and access to new technology.

A fact check: some outlets described a Cosmax–Harvard "weight-loss" project. What we can verify is a skin-microbiome research agreement with Harvard-affiliated MGH. It was signed in September 2024 and covers atopic dermatitis, acne and psoriasis, and that source does not mention weight loss. Cosmax holds about 3,000 microbial strains and about 80 related patents. (Cosmorning)

2. Dr.G heads to the US in Q4

L'Oréal said integrating Korean derma brand Dr.G strengthened North Asia, and it plans a US launch in Q4 2026. Its Dermatological Beauty division (La Roche-Posay, CeraVe, SkinCeuticals) grew 13.9% on an adjusted LFL basis in Q3 and 11.6% over 9M. (L'Oréal Finance)

  • In Q3, derma grew almost twice as fast as the group (+7.2% LFL). Korean derma brands compete in this segment.
  • US retailers should expect Dr.G to arrive with L'Oréal's distribution and marketing behind it, not as an indie test. Plan shelf space and promo calendars for that.

3. Goodai Global's IPO: what sits under the ₩7–10tn talk

Goodai Global is reportedly preparing a KOSPI listing with Mirae Asset Securities as lead manager. The market has floated a valuation of ₩7–10tn. Reports flag three valuation risks: goodwill, an ₩800bn convertible bond and the separate listing of subsidiary Craver. (The Fact / IB Tomato)

The CB was issued in August 2025 at a valuation of about ₩4.4tn. IMM PE took ₩280bn, Premier Partners ₩160bn and IMM Investment ₩140bn, reportedly with put options and target-IRR protections. (The Fact)

  • Distributors carrying Goodai brands should expect pricing and trade terms to tighten. A listing usually brings closer scrutiny of margins and channel terms.

4. Trade-fair "consultations" are not orders

According to local reports, the 2026 Osong Cosmetics & Beauty Expo runs 22–24 Oct in Cheongju with 244 exhibitors. (Nocut News) Pre-event coverage cited about 500 buyers and a target of ₩260bn in export consultations. (YTN) For comparison, the 2024 edition reportedly produced 185 contracts worth US$11.3m. (regional press)

  • The fair is still worth using to scout small brands. Ask for MOQ, lead time and certification status (CPNP, MoCRA) before samples ship.

5. India: Myntra reports K-beauty demand +200%

A Myntra report reportedly shows K-beauty demand up 200% and SPF demand up 60%, driven by ingredient-first, preventive buying among Gen Z. The period and base were not disclosed. (jay-ho.com)

  • A small base inflates growth rates, so read this for direction. Korean sunscreen looks like the easiest way into India.
