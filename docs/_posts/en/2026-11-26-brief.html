1. K-beauty: $13.65B, nearly half of Korea's consumer-goods exports

Korea Customs Service data released Nov 25 and reported by Local Segye show K-beauty exports of $13.65B for Jan 1–Nov 19, 2026, up 34.6% year over year. Total K-consumer-goods exports reached $28.65B (+21.5%), already above the 2025 full-year total of $27.02B. Beauty made up 47.6% of that, the largest single product group.

Small and mid-sized companies did most of the work. They accounted for 90.7% of consumer-goods exports ($25.99B, +23.9%), and the number of exporting firms rose 5.6% to 27,618. For buyers, the supplier pool keeps getting wider. That also makes vetting supplier capacity more important than it was a year ago.

2. Two official numbers, two scopes

A different government series gives a different figure. News1 reports that Ministry of Trade data put cosmetics exports at $12.569B for Jan–Oct 2026, with 12 straight months of growth and a total that already beats 2025. Newsis reported 2025 full-year cosmetics exports at $11.4B, and Food Today reported that October 2026 alone came to $1.444B.

  • Customs "K-beauty": $13.65B, Jan 1–Nov 19, consumer-goods classification
  • Trade Ministry "cosmetics": $12.57B, Jan–Oct, trade-statistics classification

If you put either figure in a category review or a pitch deck, cite the agency and the period. Don't mix the two on one slide.

3. The US overtakes China, but the EU grows fastest

US-bound K-consumer-goods exports reached $5.69B (+30.3%) in the same period, ahead of China at $5.1B. It is the first time since 2004 that the US has been the top destination. EU-bound exports came to $3.09B, up 55.4%, the fastest growth among major markets (Local Segye).

For European retailers and distributors, this is the window. A Korean brand can't list in the EU until CPNP notification and an EU Responsible Person are in place. Distributors who can provide RP services and handle compliance bring something to the table when negotiating exclusives.

4. Cosmax US +88%: global majors are using K-ODMs

According to CEO Score Daily and other outlets, Cosmax reported Q3 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 875.8B (+49.55%) and operating profit of KRW 83.3B (+94.93%). That is its largest quarter on record, and it came in what is usually a slow quarter. Yonhap's Korean Center reports that Korea revenue hit KRW 616.1B (+61%), while the US unit grew 88% to KRW 69.5B on more orders from global clients including L'Oréal. The US unit stayed profitable at the operating level for a second straight quarter.

There are risks too. News Tomato reports that non-operating losses are widening and debt is close to KRW 2 trillion. The China and US units are still posting net losses even as sales grow.

For buyers, the takeaway is lead times. If global majors are taking K-ODM capacity, smaller brands' production slots get tighter. Confirm H1 2027 delivery windows with your Korean suppliers now.

5. Black Friday baselines, the day before

2026 Black Friday falls on Nov 27, and no K-beauty share or ranking data is out yet. The 2025 baseline, per Hankyung: Medicube took 16.4% of Amazon beauty category sales during Black Friday 2025 (Navigo Marketing data). On Nov 30, 2025, 5 of the top 10 Beauty & Personal Care sellers were Korean brands, led by the Medicube Zero Pore Pad.

At Prime Day in July 2026, the same pad ranked No.1 in Beauty & Personal Care for the second year running, and the Anua PDRN Collagen Glow Serum Spray ranked No.6 (Apparel News). When 2026 numbers land, compare them to 16.4% and "5 of 10" before setting Q1 K-beauty orders.
