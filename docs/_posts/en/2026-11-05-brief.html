1. Boots puts K-Wellness in 50+ UK stores, and part of it is Boots' own sourcing

Boots will sell three Korean wellness brands, RIN, PrePear and Seoul Tonic, exclusively online and in more than 50 stores. It is also opening Wellness Zones in more than 30 stores (announced November 2, 2026). Retail Times

Three more ranges, Seoul Bloom, Yongsan Wellbeing and HABI, were sourced by Boots itself. BB LAB, an established Korean brand, rolls out to boots.com and 200 stores from November. Retail Times

  • Why it matters: three of the seven names are retailer-sourced. Boots is not only listing Korean brands; it is building its own Korean ranges.
  • For buyers: if you are planning a K-wellness range, compare an exclusive brand listing with a retailer-owned range made by a Korean manufacturer. Check MOQ and lead times before you commit.

2. K-beauty searches up 70%+ on boots.com, but no revenue figure

Boots says its Korean beauty sales are growing at a triple-digit annual rate and that one K-skincare product sells every seven seconds. On boots.com, K-beauty searches are up more than 70% and active K-beauty shoppers are up more than 20% year on year. Medicube searches are up more than 20% (figures from the November 2026 release). Boots did not give an absolute sales figure. Retail Times

  • For buyers: growth from a small base can look dramatic. Before you scale up, ask suppliers for sell-through and reorder data by SKU.

3. Korean ODM Soleo Cosmetic valued at KRW 36bn

Pacific Capital and Life Asset Management invested about KRW 12bn through a dedicated K-beauty fund for about 33% of Soleo Cosmetic, valuing the company at about KRW 36bn post-money. Helios PE keeps about 67% and remains the largest shareholder (reported November 2026). Dealsite

Revenue grew from KRW 20.4bn in 2023 to KRW 30.9bn in 2025. Over the same period the company moved from an operating loss to an operating profit of about KRW 1.9bn (EBITDA about KRW 2.8bn). It is targeting about KRW 36bn in revenue for 2026. Clients include The Skin Factory (Kundal), Mari&May and Wisely, and it runs a factory in Pyeongtaek. Dealsite

  • Simple math: the valuation is roughly 1.2x 2025 sales and about 13x EBITDA.
  • For sourcing teams: the client list leans on indie brands, so a few accounts drive volume. Check capacity, lead times and client concentration before you place private-label orders.

4. Dr.Jart+ reportedly signs KATSEYE's Yoonchae for North America

According to reports, Dr.Jart+, owned by Estée Lauder, named Yoonchae Jeung of KATSEYE as its first North America brand ambassador. She fronts the campaign for the Premium BB Cream SPF 30, reformulated with 2% niacinamide (announced around October 30, 2026). Cosmetics Business, Hypebae

  • Why it matters: the brand appointed a dedicated North America face instead of using a Korean-market celebrity, and tied her to one hero SKU.
  • For retailers: plan BB cream facings and inventory around the campaign window. Watch whether the reformulated hero SKU lifts the rest of the BB shelf.
