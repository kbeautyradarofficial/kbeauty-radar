The short version: North America is now the center of gravity. Q3 will show how solid the earnings are

In Q2 2026 (April–June), LG H&H's North America revenue was KRW 205.8B, up 47.3%. China came in at KRW 176.0B, down 5.0%. This is the first time North America has passed China since the company became an independent legal entity. Overseas revenue was KRW 584.5B (+12.6%), or 35% of the total. (Newsis)

On a consolidated basis, Q2 revenue was KRW 1.657T (+3.3%) and operating profit was KRW 102.8B (+87.5%). The Beauty segment posted revenue of KRW 818.4B (+3.9%) and returned to profit with KRW 44.4B in operating profit.

Why the headline numbers need a second look

  • Q2 profit includes a one-off US tariff refund. A brokerage puts it at about KRW 15B. (Newsis)
  • H1 2026 revenue was KRW 3.234T, down 2.1%. Operating profit rose 6.8% to KRW 210.6B, but one strong quarter has not yet reversed the revenue decline.
  • Right after Q2 results, DB Securities raised its target from KRW 250,000 to KRW 380,000, citing new H2 channels such as Sephora and Costco. (Korea Economic Daily) According to Newspim, its Oct 13 note set KRW 360,000, and the headline pointed to larger costs for incubating new brands. Building out channels is not free.

When Q3 results come out, check three things: Beauty operating profit without the tariff refund, how far China revenue falls, and whether North America growth stays in double digits.

Channels: Dr.Groot went Costco first, then Sephora

On Aug 15 (local time), the scalp-care brand Dr.Groot launched 14 products in 424 Sephora stores across the US. Before that, it had been sold in more than 600 Costco stores in North America since October 2025, and its Amazon sales grew 153% in H1 2026. (LG H&H)

The order is what matters. The brand proved sell-through in a value club and on Amazon first, then took that data to prestige buyers. Smaller brands can copy this sequence. They will need to split SKUs and pack sizes by channel so club pricing and prestige pricing don't collide.

Launches: Sum37's 5th generation, sold at Olive Young and department stores

On Oct 8, Sum37 released the 5th generation of its Secret Essence: 8,700 hours of fermentation and a 90% fermented base. The line has sold more than 3.35 million bottles since 2009. Pricing is KRW 57,000 for 50ml and KRW 105,000 for 100ml, through the official mall, Olive Young and department stores. (LG H&H)

The company's own clinicals show a 56% improvement in skin turnover after one week (n=31), 62.7% barrier repair after one week (n=32) and a 9% improvement in texture after four weeks (n=30). Panels are around 30 people, so each market should review these claims before they go onto export product pages.

Marketing: The Whoo built a pop-up around a single ingredient

The Whoo ran a 'The Power of NAD' pop-up at Lotte Department Store Incheon from Sep 28 to Oct 6. The centerpiece was the Bichup Jasaeng NAD Power Ampoule, launched Oct 20, 2025, which contains 50% NAD Power24™. The brand presents it as the result of 13 years of NAD research.

The play is to relaunch a year-old SKU as a hero through one ingredient story, built on two numbers: 50% concentration and 13 years of research. (LG H&H)
