1. October exports: +57.5% YoY, about +40% vs. 2024

Korea shipped $1.44B of cosmetics in October 2026, up 57.5% year over year and the highest October on record. The US led, and China and the EU also grew (customs basis). (Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources)

Read the growth rate with care. October totals were $1.03B in 2024, $0.92B in 2025 and $1.44B in 2026. Because October 2025 came in below 2024, the YoY rate is inflated by a soft base. Measured against October 2024, growth is about +40%. That is still strong, just less dramatic. (MOTIR)

  • For buyers: when a vendor pitches "+57%", ask for the two-year comparison and the brand's own sell-through data.

2. Jan–Oct exports of $12.57B already beat all of 2025

Cumulative exports for January–October 2026 reached $12.569B. That is already above the full-year 2025 total of $11.418B. (Beauty Economy)

Simple math: about $1.22B a month in November and December would take the year to $15B. That is arithmetic, not a forecast.

The US is running hotter than the total. Reports citing preliminary customs data put US-bound exports for October 1–25 at $230M, up 62.7% YoY. This is a partial-month figure. (Korea Economic Daily)

  • For buyers: lock Q1 2027 reorders with Korean suppliers this month. Year-end factory and freight capacity is tight.

3. Kolmar's US unit +122%: sunscreen capacity is the pinch point

Korean ODM Kolmar Korea posted its best quarter ever in Q3 2026. Revenue grew 21.3% and operating profit 40.5% YoY, slightly below market expectations. The US subsidiary drove growth at +122.2%, while the China and Canada units shrank. (Korea Economic TV)

One correction to the headlines: the "2x" figure refers to sunscreen inquiries, which the company says more than doubled versus prior years. It did not disclose order volumes. (Korea Economic TV)

Why it matters: in the US, sunscreen is regulated as an OTC drug, so only qualified lines can make it. If demand for Korean SPF keeps climbing, factory capacity, not consumer demand, may cap how fast these brands scale in the US. For scale, Cosmax reported Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 682B (+15.9%), and Kolmar's ODM division KRW 417.2B, according to reports. (Korea Economic Daily)

  • For buyers: confirm Summer 2027 K-SPF allocations and lead times with brands now, and ask which facility makes US-market SKUs.

4. Kids' cosmetics exports reportedly +22%, but the period is unconfirmed

Exports of children's cosmetics reportedly rose 22%, and K-baby-care brands such as Goongbe are pushing into Europe. The reference period and data source have not been confirmed. (Beauty Economy)

At home, births reportedly rose 14.5% YoY in January–August 2026, and players such as Yuhan-Kimberly are splitting newborn skincare into narrower ranges. (News1)

  • For EU retailers: ask for the CPNP notification and safety assessment documents up front. Infant products face stricter scrutiny than adult skincare.
