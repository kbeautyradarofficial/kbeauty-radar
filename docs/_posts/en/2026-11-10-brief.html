1. Glow Recipe goes to Ulta, and TikTok Shop gets it first

On Nov 10, 2026, Glow Recipe announced a nationwide launch in 1,000 Ulta Beauty stores and on Ulta.com starting Dec 6, 2026. It is the brand's first time at Ulta, nearly ten years after its Sephora debut. (PR Newswire, WWD)

The sequencing is the real story. The new Exopeptide Caffeine Fresh Slices Eye Mask ($36 for 60 patches) goes on sale only on Ulta Beauty's TikTok Shop on Nov 17, three weeks before it reaches stores. (PR Newswire)

  • The Ulta range runs $8 to $48. Glow Recipe already sells in 3,000+ doors worldwide, including Sephora, Cult Beauty and Mecca (as of Nov 10).
  • Ulta cites 46M+ loyalty members. The release gives no sales figures or rankings for the brand at Ulta.

For buyers: A social-commerce exclusive ahead of a store rollout gives the retailer three weeks of sell-through data before shelf allocation is set. When you onboard K-beauty brands, ask for a staged launch like this and let early digital velocity decide door-level depth.

2. Olive Young's US shelf: about 400 brands, many of them small Korean labels

According to CJ's newsroom, Olive Young opened its first US store in Pasadena on May 29, 2026. It carries about 5,000 products from about 400 brands, many of them small and mid-sized Korean labels. CJ reported a queue of about 400m at opening and 8M+ social views in three days. (CJ Newsroom)

  • CJ says it plans to run five stores across California and the western US by 1H 2027, then expand into eastern and south-central markets. The second store opened at Westfield Century City in June 2026. (CJ Newsroom)
  • Cosmetics Business and WWD report "five more" stores. CJ's own wording reads as five in total.
  • Citing WWD, UncoverLA reported a Torrance (Del Amo Fashion Center) store planned for fall 2026. We found no confirmation that it has opened. (UncoverLA)

For buyers: Olive Young now competes with you for K-beauty discovery in the US, and its assortment is a public scouting list. Check which indie Korean brands it carries. Expect those brands to get US distribution offers over the next two quarters.

3. Korea's regulator heads to Brazil in Feb 2027

Beauty Korea News reports that Korea's MFDS will send a K-beauty market-entry delegation to Brazil in Feb 2027, with company applications due Nov 23. We could not find the official notice. (Beauty Korea News)

The groundwork: on Feb 23, 2026, MFDS signed a regulatory cooperation MOU with Brazil's ANVISA covering cosmetics and other health-related products. Brazil sorts cosmetics into risk Grades 1 and 2 and requires Portuguese labeling. (Cosinkorea)

For Latin American distributors: Expect a wave of Korean brands pitching in February. Before you discuss MOQ, ask each one for its ANVISA grade classification and a Portuguese label draft.

4. CIIE: $170M in Korea pavilion consultations, which are not orders

According to Herald Economy and Financial News, KITA's Korea Pavilion at the 9th China International Import Expo (Shanghai, Nov 5-10, 2026) had 88 companies and logged $170M in export consultations. We could not find KITA's own release. We left out the reported "+83% participants" figure. The prior edition's pavilion had 74 companies (prior-year notice), and 83% growth does not add up against that number.

For buyers: Consultation value measures interest, not volume. If you met Korean suppliers in Shanghai, the next two weeks of sample and quotation follow-up will show which leads become real orders.
