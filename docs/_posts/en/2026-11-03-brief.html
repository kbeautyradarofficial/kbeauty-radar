1. APR: revenue +100.3%, operating margin down to about 20.8%

  • APR's preliminary consolidated revenue for Q3 2026 was KRW 773.1bn, up 100.3% year on year. Operating profit rose 68.9% to KRW 160.6bn. (SBS Biz)
  • That is an operating margin of about 20.8%, down from 24.9% in Q3 2025.
  • Overseas sales were KRW 722.9bn (+135%), or 94% of revenue. North America accounted for 46% and Europe for 23%. Together that is 69%, up from 47% a year earlier. (SBS Biz)
  • Revenue for Q1–Q3 2026 was KRW 2.134tn, already above APR's full-year 2025 total. Medicube's cumulative brand revenue has passed KRW 2tn.

For buyers: APR now earns most of its revenue in the West. The margin fell in the same quarter that North America and Europe gained 22 points of revenue share. APR has not broken out its costs. Still, a supplier spending to grow is a good counterpart to negotiate with. Settle co-op marketing terms and an in-stock plan for Q4 early.

2. Korean cosmetics exports pass full-year 2025 by October

  • Exports totalled US$12.569bn in January–October 2026, which is already more than the full-year 2025 figure. Exports have grown for 12 months in a row, according to Ministry of Trade data. (News1)
  • Reports say SMEs made up 91.3% of K-beauty exports in January–August. China/HK, the US and Europe each took roughly 20%. (CNC News)

Demand is spread across regions. Most of the volume comes from smaller brands that share the same ODM capacity. If you are planning H1 2027 replenishment, confirm MOQs and lead times with suppliers now, not in January.

3. Boots: K-beauty sales up more than fivefold

  • Boots' Beauty & Wellness Trend Report 2026 says K-beauty sales at Boots grew more than fivefold over the past year. That is one Korean skincare item sold every 11 seconds. (Asia Economy)
  • The online-only K+J Beauty Box costs £35 and holds about £140 worth of products. Brands include Beauty of Joseon, Biodance, Haruharu Wonder, Dr.Jart+ and Purito. (Asia Economy)

European retailers can use a curated box to test brands at low risk before committing to full-range listings. Before onboarding a brand, check that it has a UK Responsible Person and SPF test documentation.

4. Aekyung: the boom isn't lifting everyone equally

  • Reports say Aekyung Industrial's Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 194.2bn (+13.3%), with operating profit of KRW 4.5bn. That came after a KRW 1.6bn loss in Q1. The overseas share of sales rose to 42%. (Digital Daily)
  • The comparison base is Q3 2025, when revenue was KRW 169.3bn and operating profit was KRW 7.3bn (-23.6%). The sources we checked do not yet include a Q3 2026 result. (Digital Daily)

Strong national export totals describe the market. They say little about any single supplier's health. When vetting legacy brands, look at margin trends alongside revenue.
