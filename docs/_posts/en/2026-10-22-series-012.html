Bottom line

On October 7, the last day of Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days, K-beauty held 46% of the top 100 skincare products on Amazon US. On TikTok Shop US, one brand, Medicube, took 19% of all skincare sales in August.

These channels work differently. On Amazon, brands with many proven SKUs win. TikTok Shop drives discovery, but sales there swing hard. Measure both with the same KPI and your inventory plan will be off.

1. Amazon: nearly half the top 100, and now Europe too

  • A Kyobo Securities report, covered by Korea Credit News, puts K-beauty at 46% of Amazon US's top 100 skincare products on Oct 7. At June's Prime Day it was 38%.
  • In the overall Beauty & Personal Care (B&P) top 100, K-beauty went from 29% to 32% over the same period.
  • 18 Korean brands made the skincare top 100. Medicube had the most products (15), followed by Anua (7).
  • K-beauty share of the Amazon B&P top 100 in Europe, June vs October: Spain 18% to 38%, Italy 15% to 28%, France 14% to 25%. The UK rose 8 points and Germany 6 points.

What wins is how many SKUs each brand has in the race, not how many brands there are. Medicube alone had 15 products in the top 100. A brand that relies on one hero product gets crowded out during deal events.

2. Mid-size brands: win a subcategory first

Dongkook Pharmaceutical's Centellian24 said its sales during Prime Big Deal Days (Oct 6-7) were up 1104% on last year's event, its best result ever. Its 360 Shot PDRN Lifting Eye Cream ranked #1 among eye cream best sellers (Newspim, Womentimes).

Centellian24 did not take on Medicube across all of skincare. It picked a narrow subcategory, led with a strong ingredient keyword (PDRN) and won the #1 badge there.

A low base last year may explain part of the 1104%. The structure is the useful part: win a subcategory badge first, then let deal-event traffic find you.

3. TikTok Shop: one dominant brand, volatile sales

  • Industry News, citing Charm.io data, reports TikTok Shop US skincare sales of $75.6M in August 2026. Medicube took $14.2M of that (19%). Dr. Melaxin was second at 11%.
  • The top 10 skincare brands made up 45% of the category in August, and 8 of them were Korean.
  • Medicube's August sales fell 20% from July, and total skincare sales fell 6%. Total beauty sales still edged up $1M to $228M.
  • In Q2 2026, TikTok Shop US beauty sales were $662.2M. Skincare was the largest share at 36%.

On TikTok Shop, one video or one event can move sales sharply. Reorder off your peak month and you will be left holding stock. Use Amazon sell-through as the baseline and count TikTok Shop as upside on top.

New brands keep coming in. Dr.G launched on TikTok Shop US (The Guru). In the UK, the number of K-beauty sellers on TikTok Shop grew 875% in a year (Dong-A Ilbo). At a K-beauty-only TikTok Shop event in London in September, Beauty of Joseon's daily sales hit 4x its usual level (Etoday). A channel that can do 4x in one event can also drop back just as fast.

4. Background: exports pass $1.5B a month

According to Korea's trade ministry, cosmetics exports reached $1.51B in September 2026, up 31.4% year on year and a monthly record (CosinKorea). January-September exports totaled $11.14B (+30.8%), already 98% of the full-year 2025 figure (CNC News). MFDS data put January-September exports to the US at $2.35B (+40.6%), or 21.1% of the total, making the US the #1 destination (Newspim).

5. Risk: counterfeits

On Oct 2, Korea's SME ministry and IP office set up a joint council to fight counterfeit K-beauty on Amazon and TikTok Shop (Platum). When deal-season volume spikes, fake listings follow. Have takedown channels ready before the event, not after.

Q4 checklist

  • Lock Black Friday and Cyber Monday deal SKUs: one hero product plus 2-3 supporting products.
  • Put ad spend on listings that can reach #1 in a subcategory, such as eye cream, pads or masks.
  • Base TikTok Shop reorders on a 3-month average, not your peak month.
  • Before launching on Amazon in the EU, complete CPNP notification and local-language product pages.
  • Check trademarks and Brand Registry status, and name an owner for counterfeit takedowns.
