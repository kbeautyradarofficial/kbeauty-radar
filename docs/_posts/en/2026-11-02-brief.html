1. Amorepacific Q3: TikTok Shop UK up 200%+, but profit grows fastest at home

Amorepacific Holdings posted Q3 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 1.2073 trillion (+8.9%) and operating profit of KRW 130.1 billion (+24.7%). Operating margin rose from 9.4% to 10.8%. (Amorepacific Group IR)

  • Overseas, Amorepacific Corp. booked Q3 revenue of KRW 495.5 billion (+12%) and operating profit of KRW 48.5 billion (+14%). Domestic operating profit grew three times as fast, up 42% to KRW 84.6 billion.
  • Q3 YoY growth drivers: COSRX in EMEA +30%+, TikTok Shop UK +200%+, AESTURA in the Americas +100%+, AESTURA and COSRX in Japan +100%+ each, HERA in Japan +90%+, India +60%+.
  • The multi-brand subsidiaries (Innisfree, Etude, Espoir, Amos Professional) fell to KRW 103.2 billion in revenue (-12%) and KRW 5.2 billion in operating profit (-29%). The company attributes the drop to distribution restructuring.
  • The Korea Times reports that net profit fell 14.3% on non-operating costs.

For buyers: AESTURA and COSRX are where the overseas momentum is. In the multi-brand portfolio, expect channel cuts as restructuring continues. Plan assortments around the brands that are growing.

2. Selfridges opens permanent Biodance and Medicube counters in an eight-brand K-beauty zone

On November 2, Selfridges Oxford Street opened permanent counters for Biodance (its first anywhere in the world) and Medicube (its first in Europe). UK distributor Luneia Brands runs both counters. (TheIndustry.beauty)

  • The counters sit in the ground-floor 'The Beauty Workshop' K-beauty zone, alongside Beauty of Joseon, SKIN1004, Axis-Y, Thank You Farmer, VT Cosmetics and Dr. Reju-All.
  • Reported cumulative sales as of November 2026: more than 6 million Medicube AGE-R devices and more than 350 million Biodance hydrogel masks.

For retailers: A UK department store now treats K-beauty as a permanent counter category with device demos, not a test shelf. Because a local distributor runs the concept and staffing, the model can be repeated in other European doors.

3. Aekyung Q3: revenue up 12.5% to KRW 190.4 billion, with a KRW 3.3 billion operating loss

Aekyung Industrial's Q3 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 190.4 billion was its highest for any third quarter, but it posted an operating loss of KRW 3.3 billion. (News1)

  • MTN reports Q3 cosmetics revenue of KRW 58.7 billion (+13.9%), driven by double-digit overseas growth.
  • Asia Today reports that the company blamed the loss on spending on global ambassadors and pop-ups. Taekwang Group took effective control of Aekyung on July 1, 2026, making this its first quarter under the new owner.

For buyers: The new owner is front-loading brand spend. Before committing to larger opening orders, check whether Q4 overseas cosmetics sales show that spend paying off.

4. re:nk moves from a Costco.com sellout to US warehouse stores

According to a September 2026 company press release, re:nk's Cell to Cell Cream sold out early on Costco.com. It is slated for US Costco warehouses in late 2026, with the Cell to Cell Essence following in early 2027.

For buyers: Testing online first, then rolling into warehouses, lowers the risk of carrying a new K-beauty SKU in club stores. Ask brands for online sell-through data before you commit to in-store volume.
