1. Pharma Research: cosmetics exports +92%, operating profit +1.2%

Q3 2026 consolidated revenue was KRW 171.5bn, up 27% year on year. Operating profit was KRW 62.6bn, up only 1.2%, which works out to a 36.5% margin. (Shinailbo)

  • Q3 cosmetics revenue was KRW 62.0bn. Exports were KRW 42.1bn (+92%) and domestic sales were KRW 19.9bn (+50%).
  • About 48% of Q3 revenue came from overseas. Rejuran medical device exports were about KRW 30bn (+53%).
  • In the US, the company added Sephora doors and entered Costco. Sales in Malaysia and the Philippines rose more than 50% from the previous quarter.

In July 2026 the company reportedly agreed to acquire CG USA, a cosmetics OEM/ODM manufacturer in California, so it can make Rejuran cosmetics in the US. The plant is FDA-registered and cGMP-certified, so it can also make OTC products. (SmartToday) Some outlets reported that production will start in early 2027. No filing or investor material confirms that date.

For buyers: If a brand makes its products in the US, lead times could get shorter and landed costs more predictable. Ask your suppliers about US production plans before you agree 2027 terms.

2. Dongkook: overseas sales reportedly ~3.75x, Q3 profit down 4.1%

Dongkook's Q3 2026 consolidated overseas sales were reported at KRW 43.76bn, up 275.2% from KRW 11.66bn a year earlier. Pharmaceuticals are included in that figure, so cosmetics-only overseas sales are not reported separately. (e-focus) Q3 operating profit was reported at KRW 23.8bn, down 4.1%, and higher advertising spend was named as the cause. (News1)

As of November 2026, its skincare brand Centellian24 is reportedly sold at Costco and Ulta Beauty in North America. In Japan it is in about 7,500 stores, including Don Quijote and Loft, and it entered Europe through Boots in the UK. (Medicopharma)

For buyers: Rejuran and Centellian24 both now sell through club and prestige channels at once. If you run a specialty store, ask for channel-exclusive SKUs or pack sizes. Otherwise shoppers can compare your shelf price directly with Costco bundles.

3. Selfridges: Biodance and Medicube open counters in London

Selfridges' Oxford Street flagship now has Biodance's first permanent counter anywhere and Medicube's first counter in Europe. UK distributor Luneia Brands runs both. (BeautyMatter, reported November 2, 2026)

  • APR says Medicube Age-R devices have passed 6 million units in global cumulative sales (as of November 2026).
  • Biodance says it has sold more than 350 million hydrogel masks in total. Retail Times put the figure at 360 million.

For buyers: Here the distributor staffs and runs the counter, not the brand. Devices and masks sell better with demos and consultations. Other European department stores could use the same setup for K-beauty brands.

4. Olive Young plans to grow from 2 to 7 US stores by H1 2027

Olive Young reportedly opened its first US store in Pasadena on May 29, 2026 (8,647 sq ft). A second store followed at Westfield Century City in June 2026 (2,694 sq ft). It plans five more stores in greater LA and San Diego, including Fashion Valley, by the first half of 2027. (Chain Store Age) It reportedly also runs a North American warehouse of about 39,000 sq ft in Bloomington, California.

For buyers: Southern California is getting five more stores from a K-beauty specialist. Expect more competition there for hero SKUs and exclusive launches.
