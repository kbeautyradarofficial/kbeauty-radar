1. October exports: $1.44B, up 57.5%

Korea's trade ministry (customs basis) puts October 2026 cosmetics exports at $1.44B, up 57.5% year on year. That is the highest October on record; the previous best was $1.03B in October 2024. The all-time monthly high is September 2026 at $1.51B, so this is the second straight month above $1.4B. (Toyo Economy)

A note for buyers: customs exports, Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) data and company IR figures are defined differently. By MFDS count, 2025 exports were reportedly $11.4B, with skincare at 74.7% (Newsis). Check which basis a supplier is quoting before it goes into a range review.

2. Korea-China FTA follow-up deal: no cosmetics-specific opening

Korea and China reached a substantive agreement on the services and investment phase of their FTA on November 18, about 8 years and 8 months after talks began in March 2018. Korean travel agencies can now recruit Chinese group tourists directly, and Korean companies can run their own retail stores in China. Reports say the deal has no separate opening for cosmetics. (Newspim, Wide Economy)

The trade numbers point the same way. Kiwoom Securities data puts January-October 2026 exports to China at $1.66B, up just 2.9%, though October alone rose 31.6%, the fifth straight monthly gain. Chinese visitors to Korea reached 5.47M in January-September, already more than all of last year, and Olive Young's sales to foreign customers grew 47% in January-August. (Newspim)

Korean beauty stocks moved first: at 9:40am on November 23, Beautyskin was up 20.05% and Adbiotech up 29.81% (Newspim). Our read is that the near-term effect shows up in inbound tourist channels in Korea (duty free, Olive Young), not in China import volumes. This is not investment advice.

3. Growth is moving to the US and Europe

APR's Q3 2026 overseas revenue was KRW 722.9B, up 135%, or 94% of total sales. Europe's share of revenue went from 5% to 23%, and North America's from 42% to 46%. These figures include overseas subsidiaries and devices, so they don't match customs data one-to-one. (Toyo Economy)

Amorepacific's Q3 2026 overseas revenue was KRW 495.5B (+12%). In the US, Aestura grew more than 100% and Innisfree more than 50%; Cosrx grew more than 30% in EMEA. (Toyo Economy) LG H&H says its CNP brand put 18 products into Ulta Beauty in February 2026, with six Lipcerin SKUs in about 1,400 stores (LG H&H).

For EU retailers and distributors: Korean brands are building European distribution right now. That makes this a good time to open listing and distribution talks.

4. Hera Black Cushion: 14M units, and what '#1' actually means

Hera Black Cushion has sold 14M units since its 2017 launch (as of April 2026). The 'eight years at #1' claim rests on Kantar Worldpanel's ranking of Korean cushion purchase value from December 2018 to December 2025. The share percentage was not disclosed. (Amorepacific) The company reported passing 10M units in February 2024, which works out to about 4M more units in roughly two years (Amorepacific).

When a Korean supplier pitches a '#1' product, ask for the source, the metric (units or value), the channel and the period.
