1. Amorepacific Q3: Aestura and COSRX drive the growth

Amorepacific Group reported Q3 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 1.2073tn (+8.9% YoY) and operating profit of KRW 130.1bn (+24.7%) on October 29. CNC News Operating margin rose to 10.8%, up from 9.4% a year earlier. Lawissue

  • Japan, Q3 YoY: Aestura and COSRX each grew more than 100%, and Hera more than 90%. The Beauty Economy
  • Americas, Q3 YoY: Aestura grew more than 100% and Innisfree more than 50%. The Beauty Economy
  • The group's other beauty brands (Innisfree, Etude, Espoir, Amos Professional) combined: revenue KRW 103.2bn (-12%), operating profit KRW 5.2bn (-29%). Newsclaim

At the Amorepacific entity level, Q3 overseas revenue was KRW 495.5bn (+12%) and overseas operating profit KRW 48.5bn (+14%). Domestic operating profit grew faster: KRW 84.6bn (+42%) on revenue of KRW 620.5bn (+11%). Sidae Ilbo Entity operating profit rose 25.4% to KRW 115.2bn, but net income fell 14.3% to KRW 58.5bn. Digital Today

For buyers: In Japan and the US, the growth sits in a few hero brands, led by derma line Aestura, not in broad brand ranges. Put open-to-buy toward proven hero SKUs first. Add adjacent lines only once reorders confirm demand.

2. Olive Young's zone is in about 87% of US Sephora stores

Olive Young's "K-Beauty Edit" zone was in 580 of roughly 670 Sephora US stores, as of August 2026 reporting. Asia Economy Bisnow reports that Olive Young opened its first standalone US store in Pasadena, California, and plans five more California stores before New York. Bisnow Separately, Missha was reported in April to be expanding into about 150 Costco stores in the US, 60 in Canada and 14 in Taiwan. The Korea Times

For retailers: An Olive Young-curated K-beauty zone now sits in most Sephora US doors. If you compete for the same shopper, ask Korean brands for exclusive SKUs or value packs so your shelf doesn't duplicate that zone.

3. Neopharm: Olive Young growth at home, then US export growth

Citing an LS Securities report, Asia Economy says Neopharm's Q2 2026 exports were KRW 8.7bn (+50.0% YoY), with US exports up 65.1%. Broker estimates put its Olive Young channel sales up about 140% in the same quarter. Asia Economy

For buyers: When you screen Korean derma brands, ask for their Olive Young ranking and review history. Here, domestic traction showed up alongside export growth.

4. Olive Young app: 10.7M monthly users, but the lead is thin

Citing WiseApp/Retail data, Newneek reports that the Olive Young app had 10.7M monthly active users in September 2026 (+12% YoY), the most of any Korean beauty and fashion app. That is 430K ahead of Ably at 10.27M. Newneek

For buyers: With 10.7M monthly users, Olive Young app rankings are a large-sample read on Korean demand. Check them before you list a brand.
