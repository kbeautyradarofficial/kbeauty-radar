Bottom line

Japan is no longer a market you simply enter. It is a market where you climb inside Qoo10.

Korea's cosmetics exports to Japan reached $890M in Jan–Sep 2026, up 8.6% year on year. Over the same period, total exports grew 31.1% and US exports grew 40.6%.

The headline number matters less than the order in which a brand uses Qoo10's programs.

1. Export data: still No. 3, growing at under a third of the overall rate

  • MFDS preliminary data puts Jan–Sep 2026 exports to Japan at $890M. That makes Japan the No. 3 destination, with 8.0% of the total (Cosmorning).
  • That is $70M more than a year earlier (+8.6%). Over the same period the US reached $2.35B (+40.6%) and China $1.58B (+0.4%).
  • Total exports hit $11.1B, a record for the first nine months of any year.
  • We left out the Japan category breakdown because the reported items don't add up to the total. Check the MFDS source data before using it internally.

2. Channels: Qoo10 sales cluster in 12–13 Megawari days

  • K-beauty sells about ¥150B a year on Qoo10 Japan. A Fuji Keizai survey last year gave Qoo10 a 30% share of Japan's beauty e-commerce, and cosmetics make up 70% of the platform's sales (Bizwatch).
  • The Q2 2026 Megawari sale did ¥60.5B in GMV, up 12% year on year. eBay Japan says Megawari has grown every quarter for about six years.
  • Each Megawari runs 12–13 days a quarter. That is roughly a seventh of the quarter, but the company says its impact on sales is large.

3. Marketing case: Mega Debut gets brands in, Mega Collab grows them

  • Mega Debut is Qoo10's program for new K-beauty brands, launched last April. By August 2026, 272 brands had debuted through it, with ¥6.4B in cumulative sales as of Aug 31. It now features six brands a week (Bizwatch).
  • A simple average works out to about ¥23.5M per brand. Brands debuted at different times, so the figure is rough. It still suggests a debut alone won't build a Japan business.
  • Mega Debut sends out samples in advance to generate reviews, and it also provides livestreams and coupons.
  • Mega Collab, for brands in their growth stage, started in February 2026. It offers a 1-hour solo livestream, coupons of up to 30% and a week of prime in-app placement. Twelve brands joined in its first six months, and the company says their average GMV rose 20x from before to after the promotion.

4. Channels: the Shibuya Sample House is built to collect reviews

  • Qoo10's first offline experience space opened in Shibuya on Oct 1. It features 100+ brands, including Anua, Medicube and COSRX (Etoday).
  • Qoo10 expects about 700 visitors a day, or around 20K a month, and pitches the space as a way for Korean brands to gather local reviews.
  • A Harajuku Mega Pop-up follows in November. A flagship store of about 2,500 m² in front of Harajuku Station is planned for Q2 next year or later.

5. Launches: No. 1 spots are coming from outside skincare

  • Dalif's LPT Repair Hair Care Set hit No. 1 in the live Megawari hair ranking (The Delight, reported Sep 8).
  • Supplement brand Innerlab was picked for the Mega Debut Best 10, then ranked No. 1 in the diet category during the Encore Mega Debut (Edaily, reported Sep 21).

6. Regulation: quasi-drug ingredient standards change in October

  • From October, Japan is switching its ingredient standards for medicated cosmetics (quasi-drugs), and Korean products are covered (News1, Sep 30).
  • We could not check the transition details against the full text. If you carry whitening or acne SKUs sold as quasi-drugs, confirm the specs with the Japanese marketing authorization holder.

For buyers and retailers

  • Weight Japan orders toward Korean SKUs with proven sell-through and reorder history.
  • Use Mega Debut graduates that move into Mega Collab as a shortlist of brands with momentum.
  • Treat the 20x figure as a company-reported average, not a forecast.
  • Ask vendors to confirm that quasi-drug SKUs meet the new specs before reordering.
  • B2B: large Japanese retailers are joining the Osong Cosmetics & Beauty Expo, which opens Oct 22 (New Senior). Four Daegu beauty firms logged $3.61M in export consultations in Japan (Maeil Shinmun). Consultations are not signed contracts.

Sources
