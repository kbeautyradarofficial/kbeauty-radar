What happened: two reversals in 2024

The center of gravity for K-beauty moved in 2024. In June 2024, Herald Business reported that Korean cosmetics had overtaken France, the traditional beauty powerhouse, in the US market. Then in September 2024, CNC NEWS noted that US-bound cosmetics exports had exceeded China for two consecutive months, making the US Korea's top export destination. An export model long dependent on China began rebalancing toward the US.

Not a one-off

Coverage that followed pointed the same way. In January 2025, Chosun Ilbo described K-beauty taking the No.1 spot in the US ahead of France, with momentum spreading like dominoes to the Middle East and Europe. In October 2025, the Korea Daily (US) carried the same story of K-beauty passing France to readers in America.

Why it worked: indie brands on Amazon

In June 2025, Hit News credited indie brands' Amazon strategy for Korea's top position in US cosmetics trade. Rather than waiting for big-box distribution, smaller brands met shoppers directly on online marketplaces. The shift was driven less by conglomerates than by indie players.

What still holds: France is the next target

In June 2026, News Journalism reported that, with the US as its springboard, K-beauty had become the world's No.2 cosmetics exporter and was now strategizing to catch No.1 France.

  • The US is now K-beauty's reference market; assortments and pricing from Korean suppliers increasingly reflect US demand.
  • Growth came from indie brands selling direct online, so Amazon performance is a useful signal when screening Korean suppliers.
  • US traction is being used as proof for expansion into the Middle East and Europe, an opening for buyers in those regions.

Takeaway

The 2024 reversals were not a passing trend but a redraw of the K-beauty export map. For buyers, the question is no longer whether Korean brands can win in the West, but which US-proven brands to bring into your market next.
