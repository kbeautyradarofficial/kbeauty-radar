The short version

The most useful number this time came out of the UK. Goodai Global's next bet is on brands it builds itself. The group got big by buying brands. Now it is testing whether it can grow its own from scratch.

1. UK: TikTok Shop on top of a deep retail base

Beauty of Joseon had its best day of sales in the UK at TikTok Shop UK's "K-Beauty Super Brand Week" in London on September 12–13. The company says average daily sales over the event were about 4x its January–August 2026 daily average. 19 K-beauty brands took part, and roughly 1,000 consumers and creators attended (Money Today).

The retail base did much of the work. As of September the brand is in 1,263 Boots stores, about 760 Superdrug stores and 160 M&S stores. It entered Space NK in February 2026 and later rolled out to every Space NK store. Shoppers already knew the brand from store shelves, so the social-commerce spike landed on existing demand. The event alone didn't do this.

2. Three in-house brand tracks

  • eseve: an inner-beauty brand launched October 1. The first product, Airy Face Drink, is a low-sugar lemon sparkling ready-to-drink beverage with 435mg of enzymatically modified hesperidin per bottle. It launched first on Olive Young online and in key stores, plus Olive Better's Gwanghwamun and Gangnam Station stores. Discount and review promotions run only through October. The US and Southeast Asia come after the brand builds a sales track record in Korea (Aju News).
  • Depthi: a skincare brand being built as an in-house venture (CIC). The first product is a Bonding Barrier Cream. Launch timing and markets are not set. An earlier in-house brand, Dr.19, reportedly sold below expectations (Asia Economy).
  • PEACEMINUSONE BEAUTY: a brand reportedly co-developed with G-Dragon's PEACEMINUSONE. It targets a Korean launch in the first half of 2027, then the US and Japan. Product categories have not been disclosed (Korea Economic Daily).

Our read: each brand gets a lower-risk route. The new category opens as an Olive Young first launch to build reviews. Skincare sits in a separate in-house unit. The celebrity brand has a long runway. After Dr.19, the company is avoiding one big bet.

3. Counterfeits: the cost of scale

According to reports, since H2 2024 the company has registered its trademarks with customs authorities in about 30 countries, including Korea, the US, the EU, China, Japan, the UAE and Southeast Asia, so it gets alerts on suspect shipments. Working with an AI vendor on e-commerce and social listings, it filed 25% more takedown reports and got 31% more listings removed in H1 2026 than a year earlier (Money Today).

4. How to read the financials

FY2025 consolidated revenue was KRW 1.4717 trillion, about 4x the prior year's KRW 373 billion. Operating profit was KRW 273.4 billion (+98.6%) and net profit KRW 233.7 billion (+88.1%). The figures only count TIRTIR from its March acquisition and Skinfood and Seorin Company from August. Adding up the main subsidiaries' full-year results gives roughly KRW 1.75 trillion in revenue and KRW 401.4 billion in operating profit (NewDaily). Part of any 2026 growth will simply come from a full year of those brands. Separately, reports say former CJ Olive Young CEO Koo Chang-keun joined as co-CEO and the company is preparing an IPO (iNews24).

For buyers and retailers

  • Plan in-store and TikTok Shop events together for K-beauty brands you already stock
  • Track eseve's Olive Young reviews in October before deciding on US or Southeast Asia listings
  • Ask K-beauty suppliers for proof of customs trademark registration and how they handle takedowns
