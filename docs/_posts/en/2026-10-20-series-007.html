The short version

d'Alba Global now earns most of its revenue outside Korea: about 76% in 2Q26. Right after posting those numbers it made three moves. It reformulated its 10-year-old hero mist, launched the new version in the US before Korea, and began splitting its range into separate brands. The order of those moves says more than the headline numbers.

1. The numbers (2Q26)

According to a Kyobo Securities report summarized by StockHub, 2Q26 consolidated revenue was KRW 186.9B (+46% YoY) and operating profit was KRW 47.2B (+62% YoY), both quarterly records. By our math that is an operating margin of about 25.3%.

  • Overseas: KRW 141.5B (+74% YoY, +20% QoQ), about 76% of revenue
  • North America: KRW 34.8B (+174% YoY), helped by Amazon Prime Day
  • Japan: KRW 36.0B (+39% YoY)

In 1Q26, overseas revenue was KRW 117.7B (+85%), a 69% share (CosinKorea). The share rose 7 points in one quarter.

A Hanwha summary we could not verify reportedly puts 2Q26 Europe at KRW 20.5B (+242%) and ASEAN at KRW 22.2B (+99%). Domestic sales fell 4% to KRW 45.3B, and Russia fell 34% to KRW 12.3B on delayed shipments. The same summary says operating profit included about KRW 2B in US tariff refunds, so the 25% margin is not a clean baseline.

Reports say the company's start-of-year guidance was KRW 700B in 2026 revenue at a 21% operating margin.

The 3Q swing factor is currency. With three-quarters of sales abroad, a strong won cuts reported results. Preliminary 3Q results come out on Nov 12. Reports say Daol Investment expects a miss on FX, and an aggregator cites NH estimates of KRW 174.3B revenue and KRW 32.0B operating profit. A year earlier, 3Q25 preliminary operating profit of KRW 16.7B missed consensus (KRW 24.1B) by 30.9%, mainly on about KRW 5B of brand marketing spend (Asia Economy).

2. The launch: US first, Korea 25 days later

The White Truffle First Spray Serum Pro 2X Peptide went live on Amazon and TikTok Shop in the US on Sept 21 (PR Newswire). Active serum doubles from 5% to 10%, the oil blend goes from 4 to 15, and the formula contains 70% collagen water. Korea followed on Oct 16, positioned for wrinkle and firmness concerns among consumers in their 40s and 50s. Reports say the Naver pre-launch livestream posted the brand's best launch-day sales, but no figure was disclosed.

3. Retail: reorders and the changeover

Per Kukinews, the company targeted 6,000+ offline doors worldwide by 2026 in late 2025, including US Costco, Ulta and Target plus Costco Canada. One broker estimated about KRW 8B in offline reorders (roughly KRW 4B each from Ulta and Costco) and mentioned talks with Costco Spain/France and Boots, DM and Rossmann in Europe. Treat these as estimates.

Reformulating the hero product right before the holidays means old and new SKUs on shelf at the same time. Sell-through data splits in two, and planograms need a plan.

4. Brand strategy: moving upmarket and into adjacent categories

At its first analyst day since listing (Oct 13–14), d'Alba said it analyzed more than 1M customer reviews for the core-product renewal. It gave no financial targets (Seoul Economic TV). The roadmap, per ZDNet Korea:

  • Premium d'Alba Signature gets full marketing from 4Q26.
  • Hair becomes d'Alba Professional, with its own online store in 1Q27. A hair product already ranked #4 in sales on Qoo10 Japan.
  • A new derma brand is planned for 2027.

For buyers and retailers

  • Agree on a firm sell-out date for old SKUs before stocking Pro 2X for the holidays.
  • Watch the Nov 12 print to see whether FX or marketing spend is moving the margin.
  • Track Signature and Professional as possible line extensions.
