1. Korea-China FTA: the stock rallied, the trade data didn't (Regulation)

Korea and China declared a substantive conclusion of the FTA phase two talks on services and investment at their November 18 summit. Talks began in March 2018, so this took 8 years and 8 months. Signing is targeted for June 2027 at the earliest, with entry into force in the second half of 2027. (Etoday)

Hankook Cosmetics closed up 29.98% at KRW 6,460 on November 19. It was the only KOSPI stock to hit the daily limit that day, and it rose more than 11% again intraday on the 20th. (Aju Business Daily)

The trade data says something else. Seoul Economic TV reports that the deal has no market-opening measures specific to cosmetics, and that Korean cosmetics exports to China grew only 2.9% year on year in January-October 2026. The same report says the deal lets Korean companies with 30 or more stores in China operate them on their own. (Seoul Economic TV)

Our read for buyers: the store clause helps Korean retailers more than brands. It does not signal a near-term shift in K-beauty supply into China. Nothing changes before the second half of 2027.

2. SME cosmetics exports up 28.6% in Q3, online up 97.5% (Export data)

Korea's Ministry of SMEs and Startups puts Q3 2026 cosmetics exports from small and medium-sized companies at USD 2.79B, up 28.6% year on year. January-September exports totaled USD 7.65B, up 26.4%. SME online cosmetics exports rose 97.5% in Q3, with Europe, led by the UK and the Netherlands, growing fastest. (MTN)

Total SME exports across all sectors hit a quarterly high of USD 33.4B in Q3 (+8.8%) and USD 96.5B for January-September (+9.5%). The US has overtaken China as the largest export market for Korean SMEs. (Hankyung Business)

Cosmetics grew more than three times faster than SME exports overall in Q3. For European retailers and distributors, that means many of these suppliers already sell direct online in your market. Before you negotiate exclusivity, ask for their online sell-through data and confirm their CPNP notifications are complete.

3. Olive Young plans AI agents that build the shopping cart (Channels)

CJ Olive Young held its first tech conference, define:OMNI, in Seongsu, Seoul on November 19, with about 500 attendees including IT industry professionals. It presented five technology strategies: data-driven omnichannel, an intelligent supply chain, in-store experience, a borderless global platform, and agentic commerce, where AI helps shoppers search, compare, get recommendations and build a cart. Agentic commerce has no launch date yet. (KMJ)

Figures as announced in November 2026:

  • 24M members: 18M in Korea plus more than 6M global members
  • Global online mall revenue growing at a compound annual rate above 70% (measurement period not disclosed); the mall UI/UX will be localized by region and country
  • Foreign shoppers have spent more than KRW 1 trillion in its stores; online is over 30% of sales since the 2018 launch of its same-day delivery service; the skin-scan service has passed 1M uses

In the US, iNews24 reports a warehouse of about 3,600㎡ built in March 2026, plus a first store in Pasadena, California and a US-only online mall opened in May. A plan to reach 7 US stores by the first half of next year has so far appeared only in that report. (iNews24)

For US retailers: Olive Young is building its own US store network and online mall. Expect more Korean brands to factor their Olive Young US listing into the terms they offer you.

4. Olive Young's color store: 26% of brands are first-time listings (Channels)

On November 20 Olive Young opened Color Ground Hongdae in Seogyo-dong, Seoul: 174 pyeong of selling space across four floors. Products are merchandised by category, texture and function, not by brand. 26% of the color-cosmetics brands in the store are new to Olive Young. (Insight)

For buyers, this store is a live test bed for indie Korean color brands. The ones that sell through here are a shortlist worth checking before Olive Young gives them wider listings.

Context

Annual Korean cosmetics exports grew from USD 7.5B in 2020 to USD 11.4B in 2025. (KMJ)
